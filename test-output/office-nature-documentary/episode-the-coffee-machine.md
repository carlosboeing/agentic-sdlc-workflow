# Episode: The Coffee Machine

## Production Notes

[CAMERA: slow zoom on the polished chrome espresso machine as office workers circle with deliberate purpose]

## Narrator Observation

Here, in the fluorescent-lit savanna of the open office, a ritual as old as civilization itself unfolds. The coffee station serves as the territorial nexus where hierarchies are established and dominance is displayed through subtle yet unmistakable gestures. Watch closely as the alpha male approaches the final coffee pod—*Caffeinus desperatus*—and claims it with the casual indifference of one who answers to no authority. He extracts the precious capsule, secures his caffeine, and walks away with practiced nonchalance, leaving the empty pod box untouched. His behavior is an assertion of dominance: *comportamentum negligens*, the studied disregard of social obligation. The subordinate males who follow can only exchange glances of resignation as they discover the depleted reservoir and face the small act of defiance that will ripple through the pack for hours to come.

[FADE: slow pan across the empty pod box]
