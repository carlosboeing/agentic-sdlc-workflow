# Meet Puddles!

**Species:** Kraken Hatchling (Architeuthis Abyssalis)
**Age:** 6 months
**Sex:** Indeterminate
**Weight:** 2.3 kg (tentacle span: 45 cm)
**Arrival date:** January 14, 2026
**ID:** EYAA-2026-004

> Puddles is endlessly curious, a little ink-happy, and convinced that your arm is the best thing in the world to wrap eight tentacles around.

## About Puddles

Puddles was recovered from a commercial fishing trawler net off the coast of Norway and arrived at our Abyssal Wing dehydrated, stressed, and having already discharged roughly four liters of defensive ink onto the rescue team. Since settling in, Puddles has become one of our most engaging residents. Staff describe their grip as "the most intense hug you have ever received from anything, supernatural or otherwise." Prospective adopters should know that Puddles is currently 45 cm across and will reach an adult tentacle span of 90 to 120 meters by approximately age 200. Eternally Yours maintains a dedicated Ocean Transition Program for exactly this milestone.

## Temperament

Curious and highly social. Puddles requires several hours of daily interaction and robust enrichment -- an understimulated kraken hatchling will find their own entertainment. Not evaluated with other pets; recommended as the sole aquatic resident.

## Special Abilities

Ink defense is active and reliable. Puddles discharges ink when startled, stressed, or occasionally excited; stains are permanent on most surfaces. Suction grip (200+ cups per tentacle) can open any standard aquarium lid -- custom securing hardware is required. Chromatic camouflage is still developing; if Puddles appears to vanish, they have not escaped.

## Dietary Needs

Three feedings daily: raw herring and mackerel morning and evening, live crustaceans at midday. Deep-sea bioluminescent plankton supplement added to drinking water twice weekly is non-negotiable for tentacle development. Expect water changes after every meal.

## Housing Requirements

Minimum 2,000-liter saltwater tank, pressure-rated to 3 atmospheres. Water temperature: 4-8C, salinity 34-36 ppt. Lid must be custom-bolted. Include puzzle feeders, textured rock formations, and one shipwreck model minimum.

## Compatibility Notes

Appropriate for experienced owners with prior cephalopod experience. Smaller fish will be treated as enrichment. Children over 12 welcome at supervised feedings.

## Tank Requirements

**Minimum capacity:** 2,000 liters, pressure-rated to 3 atm
**Water temperature:** 4-8C | **Salinity:** 34-36 ppt
**Filtration:** Heavy-duty sump system rated for large cephalopods
**Lid security:** Custom-bolted, rated to withstand 15 kg upward suction force
**Lighting:** Low-intensity blue-spectrum only
**Enrichment (required):** Puzzle feeder, textured structure, shipwreck model

**Adoption Fee:** $5,000 (includes plankton supplement starter kit, lid security hardware, and one Kraken Transition Coordinator consultation)

# Meet Marina!

**Species:** Selkie Pup (Phoca Animaris)
**Age:** 8 months
**Sex:** Female
**Weight:** 18 kg
**Arrival date:** February 3, 2026
**ID:** EYAA-2026-005

> Marina has the most expressive eyes our staff have ever seen on a seal, and she has not once let anyone leave the room without giving them a reason to stay.

## About Marina

Marina arrived after a severe storm off the western coast of Ireland damaged her pelt -- the selkie's primary mechanism for shifting between seal and human form. She is currently unable to complete a full transformation; our veterinary team estimates pelt healing at six to twelve months with proper care. For now she presents as a silver harbor seal with markings that shift subtly with her emotional state. Within two weeks of arrival, Marina had become our most socially engaged Aquatic Wing resident. She positions herself at the glass nearest to whichever person appears most distressed and stays there until that person feels better. This is not trained. This is simply Marina.

## Temperament

Gentle, perceptive, and empathetic. Sensitive to emotional volatility and sudden noise. Thrives in calm, stable households. Not recommended for homes with very young children, as unpredictability is stressful for her.

## Special Abilities

Emotional attunement is passive and involuntary -- Marina reliably detects distress and responds with sustained proximity and eye contact. She can currently shift her hands and forearms to humanoid form for object manipulation. She also exhibits reliable storm sense, becoming agitated approximately 48 hours before significant weather events.

## Dietary Needs

Twice daily: fresh salmon and cod (never previously frozen), with dried seaweed crumbled over meals. Moonlight-infused water -- seawater left in direct moonlight for four hours minimum -- must be provided three times weekly. Marina is lactose intolerant; no dairy supplements.

## Housing Requirements

A 3,000-liter saltwater pool (minimum 1.5 m depth) connected to a dry haul-out area with soft bedding. Natural seawater or premium marine salt mix only. Water temperature: 10-15C. Moonlight access is required for pelt recovery; a skylight or outdoor configuration is strongly preferred.

## Compatibility Notes

Prior experience with marine mammals or aquatic supernatural species required. Does well with calm adult dogs at a distance. Best as the household's primary pet.

## Tank Requirements

**Pool minimum:** 3,000 liters, minimum depth 1.5 m
**Water type:** Natural seawater or premium marine salt mix only (no synthetic alternatives during healing)
**Water temperature:** 10-15C
**Haul-out area:** Minimum 4 sq m of dry, padded, non-slip space adjacent to pool
**Moonlight access:** Outdoor pool or skylight strongly recommended; log exposure hours for veterinary records
**Pool security:** Fence or cover required; selkies can breach barriers during storm sense episodes

**Adoption Fee:** $3,500 (includes six months of pelt healing supplements, moonlight exposure log template, and one Selkie Behavioral Specialist consultation)

# Meet Abby!

**Species:** Deep-Sea Leviathan Juvenile (Leviathan Profundus)
**Age:** Estimated 2 years
**Sex:** Female
**Weight:** Approximately 280 kg (length: 3 meters)
**Arrival date:** December 9, 2025
**ID:** EYAA-2026-006

> Abby is, by nearly every measurable standard, the most serene resident we have ever housed -- and every other animal in the facility seems to know it.

## About Abby

Abby was discovered by a deep-sea research vessel at approximately 7,000 meters depth. She showed no aggression toward the submersible; the lead researcher described her as "merely curious." She was relocated to our facility through our Abyssal Discovery Protocol and has lived here without incident for three months. Abby is a juvenile -- our Leviathan Transition Coordinator will discuss projected adult dimensions at the home assessment. Since her arrival, stress indicators for every aquatic resident within 200 meters have dropped measurably. The data is consistent.

## Temperament

Docile, deliberate, and slow to react. Abby does not startle easily and has shown no aggression. She spends approximately 20 hours per day in her cave, emerging for feedings and "observation hours" at the glass. Not recommended for first-time supernatural pet owners due to infrastructure requirements.

## Special Abilities

When startled, Abby produces a bioluminescent pulse reaching 50,000 lumens for approximately 0.3 seconds -- photosensitive eye protection is required during handling. Her calming aura has a measurable effect on stress hormones in nearby animals; adult-scale extent is undocumented.

## Dietary Needs

Abby feeds exclusively in darkness -- all lights must be off during every feeding session. Primary diet: deep-sea bioluminescent plankton (Eternally Yours partner vendors only; no approved substitute). Supplement with whole raw deep-water fish twice weekly. Add two drops of void essence per 100 liters weekly, using the provided stainless steel dropper only.

## Housing Requirements

Minimum 10,000-liter pressure-rated tank (5-10 atm). Matte black interior required -- reflective surfaces amplify bioluminescent pulses to unsafe levels. Water temperature: 2-4C. Complete darkness for 16+ hours daily. A cave structure of appropriate dimensions is mandatory.

## Compatibility Notes

Experienced aquatic-supernatural owner with prior leviathan or large deep-sea creature experience required. Sole tank inhabitant only. Not recommended for households with children due to bioluminescent pulse photosensitivity risk.

## Tank Requirements

**Minimum capacity:** 10,000 liters, pressure-rated to 10 atm
**Water temperature:** 2-4C
**Interior finish:** Matte black coating required; no reflective surfaces
**Lighting:** Blackout conditions for 16+ hours daily; light-sealed room and photosensitive shutters on observation windows
**Cave structure:** Minimum 2 m x 2 m x 2 m, reinforced
**Filtration:** Deep-sea specification recirculating system
**Observation access:** Pressure-rated ports only
**Void essence handling:** Stainless steel or borosilicate glass only; store in sealed amber glass away from light

**Adoption Fee:** $25,000 (includes tank pressure certification, three-month plankton supply, void essence starter kit, and dedicated Leviathan Transition Coordinator support)
