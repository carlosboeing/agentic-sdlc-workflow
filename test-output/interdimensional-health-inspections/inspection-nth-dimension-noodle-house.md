## Executive Summary

This follow-up inspection was conducted pursuant to Notice of Violation MFSA-NOV-2025-041. The establishment was given sixty (60) standard-dimension days to achieve compliance. Inspector Klinefeld-Voss arrived at 09:00 local time, when N equaled 7. By inspection's conclusion, N had incremented to 8, requiring real-time recalibration of all measurement equipment.

## Dimensional Stability Assessment

**Dimension N-1 (N=6 baseline):** Foundation footprint stable. No lateral extrusion detected. Rating: ACCEPTABLE.

**Dimension N (N=7 at entry):** Dining floor exhibits minor phase oscillation near the service counter. Kitchen shows residual crease memory. Rating: MARGINAL — monitoring required.

**Dimension N+1 (N=8 at exit):** Three distinct noodle filaments crossed the N/N+1 boundary during the observation window. One filament was of indeterminate length, having looped through Dimension N+2. Rating: NON-COMPLIANT.

## Corrective Actions Required

1. **Upgrade noodle containment barriers to N-adaptive specification** (MFSA-PHY-114). Replace static N=6-rated barriers with units rated to current-N plus two increments. Deadline: 30 standard-dimension days.

2. **Recalibrate portion-control mechanisms to absolute-dimensional baseline** (MFSA-LAB-055). Portion size must remain invariant regardless of any diner's dimensional state. Caloric disclosure must use MFSA Standard Mass Units. Deadline: 45 standard-dimension days.

3. **Adopt approved temperature scale valid across all accessible dimensions** (MFSA-TMP-009). Current Kelvin-N units no longer recognized by this Authority. Deadline: 60 standard-dimension days.

Failure to remediate within specified deadlines will result in escalation to MFSA-NOV-2026 status and potential suspension of inter-dimensional operating license.
