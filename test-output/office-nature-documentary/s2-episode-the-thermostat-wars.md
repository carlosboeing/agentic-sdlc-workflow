# Episode 2: The Thermostat Wars

## Narrator Observation

In the modern office ecosystem, a sacred totem presides over the climate control ritual. The specimen designated *Homo corporatus frigidus*—the parka-wearer—observes the thermostat with reverence, interpreting its settings as crucial to survival. Yet the opposing phenotype, *H. corporatus tropicus*, clings desperately to their desk-mounted fan at maximum velocity, challenging the totem's authority with quiet desperation. The thermostat itself remains indifferent, a monument to eternal corporate conflict that neither side shall ever truly conquer.
