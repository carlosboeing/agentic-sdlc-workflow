# Visitor Guide: Terran Dining Protocols

**Classification:** UNCLASSIFIED -- FOR TOURIST USE
**Document No.:** GFBTT-CUL-2026-002
**Issuing Authority:** Department of Cultural Xenology (co-issued with Department of Hazardous Consumables), Galactic Federation Bureau of Terran Tourism
**Effective Date:** Galactic Standard Date 7741.3 (Terran: March 15, 2026)
**Distribution:** All registered Terran visitors
**Supersedes:** None

---

## 1. Designated Consumption Zones

Earth humans perform nutritive intake at facilities called "restaurants," where specialist humans transform organic matter into configurations others find palatable. Visitors pay for this service, then pay again via informal supplemental tax (see Section 3).

Entry requires advance reservation. Without one, humans congregate at the entrance performing suppressed-frustration rituals. Emulate this behavior precisely.

## 2. Menu Translation Guide

Upon seating, staff present a laminated document listing available consumables. **Warning:** names frequently bear no relationship to contents.

| Menu Item | Actual Contents |
|---|---|
| Hot Dog | Processed mammal offcuts in a bread cylinder; no canine matter present |
| Chicken Fingers | Breaded avian flesh; avians do not possess fingers |
| Loaded Potato Skins | Potato exteriors with dairy and pork; item is not armed |
| Deconstructed Salad | Salad ingredients placed separately; rationale unavailable |
| Death by Chocolate | Cocoa confection; not lethal to humans; hazardous to select species |
| Eggs Benedict | Poached ovum on bread; named after a traitor for unrecorded reasons |

## 3. The Tipping Protocol

Upon completion, an additional informal payment — a "tip" — is expected. Formally optional; socially mandatory. Non-compliance incurs lasting social censure.

| Region | Standard Tip | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| United States | 18–20% | Non-tipping considered a moral failing |
| United Kingdom | 10–12.5% | Service charge may be added without disclosure |
| Japan | 0% | Tipping is offensive; do not tip |
| Australia | 10% (optional) | "Optional" is not optional |
| France | Round up + 5% | Server emotional state irrelevant |

## 4. Beverage Advisory

Before ordering beverages, consult Advisory GFBTT-HAZ-2026-001 for any substance described as "coffee." Hazardous Consumables protocols supersede standard dining guidance for psychoactive beverages.

## 5. Departure Protocol

Humans do not depart upon finishing intake. A 15–40 minute socialization period is expected. Early departure signals emergency or dysfunction. Visitors should remain seated reviewing GFBTT documentation for this interval.
