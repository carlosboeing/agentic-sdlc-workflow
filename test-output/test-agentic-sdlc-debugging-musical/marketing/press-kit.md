## Behind the Scenes

*The Debugging* began as an offsite exercise: explain our worst sprint in musical form. Priya Mehta's 47-page PRD -- written on a napkin, then transcribed -- became the libretto. The Legacy Codebase ensemble is composed of contemporary dancers, each trained to embody a specific broken module. The Auth Service dancer rehearsed entering from the wrong wing for six weeks.

---

## Show Synopsis

*The Debugging* opens at a pitch competition where Jordan Chase, CEO of CtrlZ, launches into "The Pitch" (Act I, Scene 1) -- a jazz-hands manifesto promising to disrupt the undo market. The investors are baffled but intrigued.

At standup the next morning, Priya Mehta leads Marcus Chen, Zoe Kim, and Aisha Okafor through "The Standup Ballad" -- a power ballad where ticket updates become confessions and the pipeline stays red.

Act I, Scene 3 erupts when Marcus and Zoe both push to the same file. Their argument becomes a tango -- "The Merge Conflict" -- before exploding into the ensemble rock anthem "Rebase or Rewrite?" Riley Park, The Intern, closes the act with "The Intern's Lament" (Act I, Scene 4): six months into a one-day CI fix, acoustic guitar, high tenor, still waiting for the green light.

Act II escalates through demo failure and a graceful rollback waltz before the climax: "Technical Debt (The Musical Number)" (Act II, Scene 3), where The Legacy Codebase performs increasingly brittle choreography. When the Auth Service stumbles, the chain reaction is breathtaking. The team ships anyway. The tests pass.

The finale reprises "The Pitch" melody in "Ctrl+Z (The Reprise)": same tune, new lyrics. You cannot undo the people. You cannot rollback the team. The full cast -- investors included -- closes in G major.
