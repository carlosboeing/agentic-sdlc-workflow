# The Office: A Nature Documentary
## Season 2, Episode 3: The Lunch Thief

---

The communal refrigerator stands at the heart of the habitat — a shared resource governed not by written law, but by an ancient, unspoken treaty. Within its humming interior, each container is a territorial claim, each labeled bag a declaration of sovereignty. The arrangement is fragile, and it relies entirely on trust.

[SFX: Low, ominous refrigerator hum fades into silence]

On this Tuesday, the treaty is broken. A specimen of the subspecies *Homo officinalis lamentosus* opens the refrigerator to discover that its midday sustenance has vanished — triggering the distress display known as *lamentatium sandwichii*: a long exhale, a slow pivot, searching glances at colleagues. [CAMERA: Hold on empty shelf, zoom slowly] Forensic analysis reveals only a smear of mustard, and within the hour, a passive-aggressive note is deployed with surgical precision.

The aftermath escalates swiftly: an all-staff email, subject line *Re: Common Courtesy*, propagates through the colony, and a cardboard box bearing the hand-lettered inscription "CAMERA" appears atop the refrigerator. [SFX: Surveillance beep, unconvincing] A cold peace settles over the kitchen — uneasy, watchful, and richly passive.
