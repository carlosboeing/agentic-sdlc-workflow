# Interdimensional Pet-Proofing Your Home

Bringing a supernatural pet home is one of the most rewarding decisions you will ever make — and one that requires preparation your standard guide does not cover. Childproofing latches and removing toxic plants still matter, but your new companion may also phase through your bedroom wall, slip into a void pocket behind the refrigerator, or trigger a local temporal event. These four precautions are non-negotiable before your pet arrives.

---

## Portal Sealing

Naturally occurring dimensional portals form most often in enclosed, low-traffic spaces: the back corners of closets, beneath staircases, and behind large mirrors, which act as resonance surfaces for dimensional membranes. For a supernatural pet, an unsealed portal is an unsupervised exit.

**Steps:**
1. Walk each room with the Eternally Yours Portal Detection Wand (included in your welcome kit). Active portals produce a low audible hum and faint iridescence.
2. Seal active portals with Dimensional Threshold Sealant (available from Eternally Yours partner vendors). Apply two coats; the portal collapses within 72 hours.
3. Contact your home insurer to add a Supernatural Premises Rider. Standard homeowner policies exclude dimensional breach events, and an unsealed portal is a premises liability.

Inspect for new portals every six months, particularly around solstices.

## Void-Pocket Prevention

Void pockets are small tears in the fabric of reality — typically no larger than a dinner plate — most common in homes built before 1950. They appear as a faint silvery distortion, slightly cooler to the touch than surrounding surfaces. For supernatural pets, void pockets are structural defects, not doorways: a pet that enters one risks partial phasing, accidental dimension-hopping, or temporal displacement.

**Steps:**
1. Use the Eternally Yours Void Pocket Detection Kit. The treated paper strips change color within 30 seconds near an active void pocket.
2. Check along baseboards, under sinks, inside closet floors, and behind appliances — the highest-incidence locations.
3. Seal confirmed void pockets with Fabric-of-Reality Filler (included in the kit). Standard wall filler has no effect. Allow 48 hours to cure.

Do not seal any void pocket larger than 30cm across yourself. Contact Eternally Yours for a referral to a licensed dimensional contractor.

## Temporal Displacement Alarms

Temporal displacement occurs when a supernatural pet slips forward or backward in time — typically by minutes, occasionally by hours. Left undetected, your pet may arrive home before they left, or not arrive at all. Warning signs: objects near your pet appearing to blur, the pet leaving and re-entering the same room repeatedly, or a persistent faint ticking with no source.

**Steps:**
1. Install Eternally Yours Temporal Displacement Alarms on each exterior door and at least one interior door per floor. They are battery-powered and calibrate to your local temporal baseline during setup.
2. If an alarm triggers, do NOT attempt to follow or retrieve your pet yourself. Call the Emergency Temporal Hotline: **1-800-555-TIME** (24 hours). Trained specialists will locate and return your pet safely.
3. Do not reset the alarm until a specialist confirms the displacement has resolved.

## Phase-Through-Walls Protocol

Some supernatural pets can phase through solid matter — involuntarily during sleep, excitement, or stress. Apply Dimensional Anchor Film to the interior surfaces of your bedroom, kitchen, and front door, in that order of priority. The transparent film creates a reality-stabilization field that makes phasing through treated surfaces significantly more difficult.

If your pet phases into a neighbor's home, call your neighbor before they call animal control. Most pets can be guided back calmly within a few minutes with a familiar treat.
