# Proxima Station -- The Crossroads of Commerce

## Overview

Proxima Station orbits Proxima Centauri as the galaxy's most lucrative (and least regulated) trading hub. Despite complaints from BITS, the Bureau of Interstellar Tourism and Safety, the station remains a magnet for merchants seeking goods unavailable through legitimate channels.

## Climate

The station maintains artificial atmosphere in its commercial core, though periodic thermal fluctuations during solar flares have caused minor incidents. Visitors are advised to keep emergency suits accessible at all times.

## Attractions

- Merchant's Nexus -- sprawling marketplace with authentic Andromedan silks and suspiciously portable cloaking devices (200 GC)
- The Vault -- black-market establishment offering rare isotopes and starship components
- Observation Decks -- legitimate tourist venues featuring Proxima system views and overpriced beverages

## Safety Rating

**2/5** -- Transport infrastructure is sturdy and crime economically discouraged, though traders report occasional disputes settled outside official channels.

## Bureau Advisory

BITS acknowledges Proxima Station's existence and regrets its inability to enforce jurisdiction. Visitors assume all personal, financial, and legal risks.
