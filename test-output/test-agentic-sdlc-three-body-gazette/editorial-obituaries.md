### GUEST EDITORIAL: The Dark Forest Does Not Forgive Hesitation

*By Elder Shu-Rong, Civilization Advisory Council, Civilization 203*

I have survived eleven Chaotic Eras. I have watched four civilizations collapse around me — including Civilization 200, which was reduced to eleven survivors before the Stable Era returned. I say this not to claim authority, but to establish what I know: Trisolaris has always been on the edge of extinction. Every stellar cycle of survival has been borrowed against the next catastrophe.

The Dark Forest theory is not philosophy. It is physics. The universe is not silent because it is empty. It is silent because every civilization that announces itself is destroyed. To broadcast your existence is to invite annihilation from hunters you cannot see and cannot negotiate with. The only rational response upon locating another civilization is to act first.

I understand the weight of what we propose. Earth is inhabited. Its people have not threatened us. But the Sophons' intelligence makes clear that Earth's scientific capacity, left unchecked, will surpass our own within a thousand stellar cycles. At that point, the question will not be whether we survive — it will be whether we are even remembered.

Our fleet departed. One thousand and four ships carrying our civilization's continuation into a 450-stellar-cycle journey. That departure was not an act of aggression. It was an act of species memory — the memory of Civilizations 1 through 202, whose silence now fills the crust below our feet.

We have survived 203 civilizations. We will not fail in our 204th by hesitating.

*— Elder Shu-Rong, Civilization Advisory Council, Civilization 203*

### OBITUARIES

**Ren Zhao-Kai**, 1,204 stellar cycles, perished during the tri-solar syzygy of Cycle 847-Current, which reached peak alignment at 1847 stellar time. Ren Zhao-Kai was in transit from Sector 7-North to deep-crust shelter Level 9 when syzygy-level gravitational forces exceeded structural tolerance thresholds. Despite holding valid evacuation routing documents, Ren was unable to reach shelter within the required window. Surface temperature at time of death was estimated at 9,400°C. Ren is survived by three dehydrated family members stored at Facility C-4, District 6. Memorial service will be held during the next Stable Era, date to be confirmed by the Bureau of Stellar Forecasting. No rehydration required for attendance.

---

**Fen Hua-Lin**, 634 stellar cycles, died of acute dimensional stress during the Sophon deployment ceremony, which concluded with the first successful 9-dimensional proton fold following 2,187 prior attempts. Fen Hua-Lin, a junior Sophon operator assigned to dimensional monitoring under Lead Engineer Deng Yurun, sustained fatal biophysical damage during the stabilization phase when the unfolding into a two-dimensional plane propagated faster than modeled. Colleagues described Fen as meticulous and reliable. Fen is survived by one sibling, currently dehydrated in storage at the Trisolaran Academy of Advanced Materials residential annex. No memorial service is planned, consistent with Fen's documented preference. Fen's operational records have been archived by the Academy for training purposes.
