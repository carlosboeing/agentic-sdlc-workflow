# Meet Cinder!

**Species:** Phoenix (*Ignis Renascens*)
**Age:** 47 life cycles (current cycle: approximately 12 years)
**Sex:** Male
**Weight:** 4.8 kg (variable within 48 hours of rebirth)
**Arrival date:** January 14, 2026
**ID:** EYAA-2026-001

> Cinder is a gentle, patient soul who emerges from every rebirth with the same soft, inquisitive tilt of his head.

## About Cinder

Cinder arrived at Eternally Yours after his family of nine years surrendered him with genuine heartbreak — three young children and no dedicated nesting room made each combustion event too disruptive to manage. He carries no behavioral issues from that placement. Our counselors find him most settled when his routine is consistent and he has a single trusted person at home. Between cycles, he is calm and affectionate, fond of sunny windows and gently preening the hair of anyone who sits quietly beside him. In the 24 hours before a rebirth event, he becomes restless and warmth-seeking — a predictable pattern that experienced owners manage without difficulty.

## Temperament

Best suited to a quiet adult household. Bonds strongly to one primary caretaker. Moderate energy with active morning periods. Has never shown aggression in our care.

## Special Abilities

- **Cyclical combustion and rebirth**: Every 28–35 days, reaching 1,400°C for approximately 90 seconds. Rebirth completes within 4–6 hours.
- **Passive radiant warmth**: Resting surface temperature of 55–65°C. Standard heat-resistant gloves recommended for handling.
- **Ash generation**: Approximately 200g of non-hazardous fine ash weekly.

## Dietary Needs

Volcanic mineral supplements mixed into high-calcium charcoal pellets, served at a minimum of 200°C. Cinnamon bark offered as a comfort treat. Fed twice daily; partner vendor starter kit included at adoption.

## Housing Requirements

Dedicated nesting enclosure (minimum 2m × 2m × 2m) with heat-resistant bedding. Nesting room must be rated for 1,400°C flash events. Three supernatural-rated smoke detector units are included in the adoption package.

## Compatibility Notes

Recommended for experienced supernatural pet owners. Not compatible with young children, open paper storage, or standard fire detection systems.

## Fire Safety Warning

The rebirth event produces a 90-second, 1,400°C flash — every room Cinder accesses must have fire-rated walls and flooring. Standard homeowner's insurance does not cover phoenix-related damage; ISFA-approved insurer referrals are provided at adoption. Do not attempt to interrupt a combustion event. Maintain a 2-meter clearance around the nesting enclosure during pre-combustion restlessness. A Supernatural Pet Registration letter for your local fire authority is provided at no additional cost.

## Adoption Fee

**$2,500** — includes three supernatural-rated smoke detectors, a 30-day supply of volcanic mineral supplements and charcoal pellets, cinnamon bark starter pack, and one pair of heat-resistant gloves.

# Meet Flicker!

**Species:** Fire Salamander (*Salamandra ignis*)
**Age:** Approximately 3 years (estimated lifespan: 400+ years)
**Sex:** Female
**Weight:** 280g
**Arrival date:** February 3, 2026
**ID:** EYAA-2026-002

> Flicker takes her time with new people — but once she has decided you are worth trusting, she will spend every evening curled against the warmest part of your arm.

## About Flicker

Flicker was recovered as a hatchling from the cooling vents of a dormant volcano in Iceland and has been in Eternally Yours' care since she was approximately six weeks old. She has never had a permanent home, and our staff have invested considerable time building her confidence with human contact — work she has met with quiet determination. On first meeting, she retreats to the warmest corner of her enclosure. With patient, consistent handling at her pace, she warms considerably. Shelter veterinary staff confirm her health is excellent: skin temperature steady at 85°C, coloration vibrant amber-black, no signs of dietary stress.

## Temperament

Independent but affiliative — she prefers to be near her person rather than held constantly. Low-to-moderate energy; most active in the early morning and late evening. Her defensive response to perceived threat is a brief heat pulse, which diminishes with established trust.

## Special Abilities

- **Passive heat emission**: Constant skin surface temperature of 85°C. Heat-resistant gloves required for all handling.
- **Heat pulse defense**: Reflexive 120°C surface pulse when startled; non-aggressive and decreases with trust.
- **Environmental compression**: Can pass through gaps as small as 2cm — enclosures must be fully sealed.

## Dietary Needs

Live fire beetles, ember moss, and a weekly sulfur crystal supplement. Basking surface maintained at 120°C required. Fed every other day; overfeeding causes lethargy. Fresh water not required — Flicker hydrates through heat absorption.

## Housing Requirements

Sealed terrarium with volcanic rock substrate, heat lamps maintaining a minimum ambient of 65°C, and a lava pool (magma substitute available from partner vendors). No gaps larger than 2cm anywhere in the enclosure. Minimum size: 60cm × 40cm × 40cm.

## Compatibility Notes

Not compatible with aquatic pets or cool-environment species. Suited to owners comfortable with reptile-style husbandry who will continue her socialization work.

## Fire Safety Warning

Flicker's resting skin temperature of 85°C poses a burn risk to unprotected hands — heat-resistant gloves are mandatory for all handling, no exceptions. The 120°C basking surface must not be accessible to children or non-fire-adapted pets. Terrarium substrate reaches temperatures that will ignite standard organic materials; keep all paper, fabric, and wood at least 30cm from the enclosure. In the event of an escape, do not attempt retrieval with bare hands — contact the Eternally Yours emergency line immediately.

## Adoption Fee

**$850** — includes starter supply of fire beetle food, ember moss, and sulfur crystals; one pair of heat-resistant gloves; and a detailed husbandry guide.

# Meet Spark!

**Species:** Ember Sprite (*Igniculus vagans*)
**Age:** Approximately 1 year (lifespan: indeterminate — sprites do not appear to age)
**Sex:** Indeterminate
**Weight:** 12g (approximate; fluctuates with ambient temperature)
**Arrival date:** March 1, 2026
**ID:** EYAA-2026-003

> Spark lights up a room — sometimes literally — and has not yet met a person they did not immediately want to investigate.

## About Spark

Spark was found in the cold ashes of a campfire in the Cascade Range in late February — hypothermic, their flame reduced to a faint glow — and brought to our Ember Fields facility for recovery. They are now fully restored and operating at peak social energy. Ember sprites become genuinely distressed when left alone for more than two hours, which manifests as uncontrolled flare-ups. Spark's flare-ups have decreased substantially since arrival, but prospective adopters must honestly assess their capacity for near-constant companionship before committing.

## Temperament

Exuberant, curious, and deeply social. Follows their person room to room and investigates anything new. Has a documented fondness for igniting paper products — playful, not malicious. Most active in the evenings; settles readily in warm, quiet environments.

## Special Abilities

- **Free flight**: Moves freely through the air; cannot be contained by standard enclosures.
- **Heat absorption**: Feeds entirely by absorbing heat from open flames. Does not eat solid food.
- **Thermal communication**: Steady amber glow indicates contentment; rapid white pulses signal excitement or distress.
- **Stress flare-ups**: Isolation beyond two hours triggers brief 200°C pulses.

## Dietary Needs

Spark requires a controlled open flame at all waking hours — a gas fireplace, glass-surround candle station, or the Eternally Yours Sprite Feeding Hearth. Supplement with a pinch of charcoal dust in the flame twice daily. No solid food or water.

## Housing Requirements

Spark is free-roaming and requires no enclosure. The home must be comprehensively fireproofed before arrival: fire-rated flooring and wall coverings, all paper and fabric in closed fire-rated storage, electrical cords in metal conduit. Spark sleeps in warm spots — fireplaces, radiators, behind appliances — and those areas must be clear of flammable materials.

## Compatibility Notes

Compatible with calm adults and older children (12+). Not recommended for homes with infants, non-fire-rated furnishings, or owners who travel regularly.

## Fire Safety Warning

Comprehensive home fireproofing is required before Spark arrives — Eternally Yours will not approve homes that do not meet ISFA standards. All paper, fabric, and standard upholstery must be removed from sprite-accessible areas before move-in day. Install supernatural-rated smoke detectors throughout. Do not leave Spark unsupervised for more than two hours; arrange a trusted caretaker for any longer absence. Our support hotline is available at any hour for flare-up emergencies.

## Adoption Fee

**$400** — includes a 30-day supply of charcoal dust, one Sprite Feeding Hearth candle kit, and a home fireproofing assessment by Eternally Yours staff.
