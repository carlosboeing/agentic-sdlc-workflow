# Act II, Scene 1: Demo Day

*The CtrlZ pitch room. Same investors. The projector reads: "CTRLZ: LIVE DEMO." Error messages scroll across the backdrop. Red warning lights flood the stage.*

**Characters present:** Jordan Chase, Priya Mehta, Marcus Chen, Investors (ensemble)

---

**JORDAN:** *(sweating but smiling)* Investors, today you'll see the fully working product.

*The demo loads. A spinner. Then: `500 Internal Server Error`.*

**INVESTOR 1:** Is the error part of the demo?

**JORDAN:** That — yes. "Transparent Failure Mode."

*Priya steps forward into spotlight.*

---

### Song: "It Worked on My Machine"

**Performed by:** Priya Mehta (solo)
**Style:** Blues number, spotlight
**Key:** B-flat minor
**Tempo:** 68 BPM (Slow blues)

**[VERSE 1]**
I tested at eight a.m., the build was green and clean,
Every edge case passing — the smoothest run you've seen.
But the auth-service broke — someone merged a war —
Marcus and Zoe's tango left conflict at the door.

**[CHORUS]**
It worked on my machine, it worked on my machine,
Unresolved `<<<<<<< HEAD` markers poisoned the routine —
Production hates me, staging lied —
It worked on my machine and now I want to cry.

**[BRIDGE]**
Page thirty-three: "Resolve before you merge."
Forty-seven pages. No one read them. This. Was. The surge.

**[FINAL CHORUS / TAG]**
It worked on my machine — Jordan's pivoting, server's down —
It worked on my machine and now we're losing the crown.

*The demo crashes. Screen goes black. Investors write notes.*

# Act II, Scene 2: The Rollback Waltz

*The office. Investors gone. Blue wash replaces the red. The team gathers.*

**Characters present:** Jordan Chase, Priya Mehta, Marcus Chen, Zoe Kim, Aisha Okafor, Riley Park

---

**MARCUS:** v1.4.2. Four weeks back. Before the merge war.

**AISHA:** *(checking her spreadsheet)* Filed it as P1. Ticket CTZ-881. Nobody assigned it.

*A waltz begins. The team pairs off — each couple a service reverting: Marcus and Zoe as `auth-service`, Priya and Jordan as `undo-core`, Aisha and Riley as `analytics-layer`.*

---

### Song: "The Rollback Waltz"

**Performed by:** Full cast
**Style:** Waltz, 3/4 time, elegant and bittersweet
**Key:** D major
**Tempo:** 84 BPM (Moderato waltz)

**[VERSE 1 — Marcus and Zoe]**
*One, two, three — revert, two, three —*
The hash we're returning to is where we used to be.
Before the tango, before the conflict, back to what was clean.

**[CHORUS — full cast, turning in pairs]**
Roll it back, roll it back,
The new release broke the track —
Not a failure, just a step
To the version we still kept.
Roll it back, to the place before the crack.

**[BRIDGE — Aisha, deadpan]**
I said this in the retrospective. And the one before that.

**[FINAL CHORUS / TAG — full cast, slowing]**
Roll it back... roll it back...
The waltz is slower now, the logs are turning green —
Roll it back to somewhere stable, roll it back to clean.

*The blue light steadies. A green terminal cursor blinks upstage.*

**MARCUS:** Build successful. All tests passing.

**RILEY:** *(quietly)* It worked.
