# Sea of Tranquility, the Moon — Apollo 11 Moon Landing

**Rating:** ★★★★★
**Reviewer:** CosmicBackpacker_77
**Date Visited:** 20 July 1969
**Date Reviewed:** 15 March 2026
**Trip Type:** Solo

---

## Review

Look, I'll be honest: this is a five-star review and I'm still not sure how to explain my complaints. The Moon is objectively the most awe-inspiring destination I've visited across any timeline. I watched Neil Armstrong take humanity's first step onto another world. I saw Earth hanging in a black sky like a blue marble on velvet. I literally cried inside my helmet, which was deeply uncomfortable and also deeply moving.

That said.

The spacesuit is not flattering. I get that it's a life-support system and not a fashion statement, but there is no good angle. After the first hour of bouncing around (admittedly delightful), there is **literally nothing to do**. No restaurants. No nightlife. No cell service — not a single bar. I asked one of the astronauts if there was a gift shop and I don't think he found it as funny as I did.

The flag-planting ceremony was a moment I'll treasure forever, but it dragged on. It felt like a very long photo op. I've attended shorter ribbon-cuttings for regional strip malls.

What I will say is that NASA pulled this off with 1960s technology. I've seen better computing power in a smart toaster. The sheer audacity is genuinely staggering.

**Travel Tips:**
- Bring snacks. Significant oversight on my part.
- The return trip is bumpy. Brace yourself.
- Do NOT attempt to take moon rocks through temporal customs. Learn from my experience.
