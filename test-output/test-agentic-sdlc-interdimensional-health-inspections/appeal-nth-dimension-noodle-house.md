# Appeal Ruling: The Nth Dimension Noodle House

**Inspector/Author:** Appeals Panel — Chair Commissioner Elara Voss-Hendrick, Badge 00-APL
**Dimension:** All applicable dimensions

## Case Summary

The Nth Dimension Noodle House (the Appellant) filed a formal appeal of violations cited in Follow-Up Inspection Report MFSA-INS-2026-003. The Appellant contests the applicability of MFSA standards to variable-dimension establishments and requests dismissal of all outstanding corrective action directives.

## Appellant's Arguments

The Appellant contends that MFSA regulations were codified for fixed-dimension establishments. Requiring containment barriers rated to a static N-value is physically unachievable when N changes continuously during operation.

## MFSA's Response

The Authority maintains that food safety obligations are independent of a premises' topological complexity. Consumer-protection requirements apply universally regardless of the Appellant's operational geometry.

## Findings of Fact

1. Inspection Report MFSA-INS-2026-003 documents that noodle filaments crossed the N/N+1 dimensional boundary on at least three occasions during the inspection window.
2. Portion sizes are demonstrably inconsistent relative to ambient dimensional conditions, violating MFSA-LAB-055 caloric disclosure requirements.
3. The Appellant's containment barriers are rated to N=6 and do not account for incremental dimensional change.
4. The Appellant has not contested the factual findings of the Report, only their legal applicability.

## Legal Analysis

Under Section 14(c) of the Multiversal Food Safety Act, compliance obligations attach to any establishment serving consumables to sentient beings across any number of dimensions. The Act does not exempt variable-geometry premises. The panel acknowledges the Appellant's argument has merit as a policy matter, but lacks authority to grant a categorical regulatory exemption; that remedy lies with the MFSA Legislative Review Committee.

## Ruling and Order

The appeal is **DENIED IN PART and HELD IN ABEYANCE IN PART**.

1. Violations MFSA-PHY-114 and MFSA-LAB-055 are upheld. The Appellant must install N-adaptive containment barriers and recalibrate portion-control systems within ninety (90) standard-dimension days.
2. Violation MFSA-TMP-009 is held in abeyance pending the MFSA Legislative Review Committee's evaluation of Kelvin-N unit recognition within thirty (30) standard-dimension days.
3. The Appellant may petition the Review Committee directly on the broader regulatory framework question.

*Issued by the MFSA Appeals Panel, Variable-Geometry Division.*
