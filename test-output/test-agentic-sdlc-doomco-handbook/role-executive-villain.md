# Role Profile: Executive Villain (C-Suite)

**Department:** Executive Villainy & Strategic Doom

---

## Position Summary

The Executive Villain provides strategic oversight for all doom-related initiatives and ensures quarterly destruction targets are met on schedule. This role maintains operational continuity across all nefarious business units while projecting calm menace during stakeholder presentations.

## Required Evil Skills

- Proven ability to deliver monologues of 3-5 minutes without losing the room
- Advanced threat articulation across global and interdimensional markets
- Competency in cape management and dramatic silhouette positioning
- Track record of recovering from hero-related project interruptions
- Executive-level experience with doomsday device budget reconciliation

## Common Workplace Violations

- Revealing the full plan before defenses are activated
- Leaving unconscious heroes unsupervised near control panels
- Approving overtime during active infiltration windows

## Evil Performance Metrics

| Metric | Target |
|---|---|
| World Domination Progress | 40% by Q3 |
| Monologue Completion Rate | 95% uninterrupted |
| Escaped Hero Incidents | fewer than 2 per quarter |

## Monologue Requirements

- Must open with a rhetorical question directed at captive audience
- Minimum one reference to personal backstory involving a perceived slight
- Must conclude before pressing any activation buttons
- Gesturing approved; turning away from hero is not

## Direct Reports

- **Mad Scientist** -- Oversees device development and laboratory compliance
- **Henchman (General)** -- Manages field operations and synchronized patrol scheduling
