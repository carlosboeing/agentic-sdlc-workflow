# INCIDENT REPORT

**Report No:** FT-HR-003
**Date Filed:** 2026-03-14
**Filed By:** HR Department, FairyTale Corp
**Subject:** The Wicked Witch of the West, Senior Chemist
**Violation Category:** Unauthorized Magic Use in Common Areas

## Summary

On 2026-03-12, the subject used the breakroom microwave to brew potions, resulting in: (a) green mist triggering the smoke alarm three times, (b) a communal coffee mug transformed into a frog, still at large, (c) two colleagues temporarily rendered invisible, and (d) the breakroom declared a hazmat zone by Facilities.

## Witness Statement

> "The frog has been observed at the water cooler and seems to be enjoying its new life."
> — Rapunzel, Facilities Manager

## HR Recommendation

The subject is suspended from breakroom privileges for 30 days, required to complete No-Magic-in-Common-Areas re-training, and referred to the Enchantment Ethics Board.

*This report is filed under the FairyTale Corp Workplace Conduct Policy, Section 12.3*
