# Mirrorghast (*Phantasma specularis*)

## Classification

**Phylum:** Phantasma
**Family:** Specularantidae
**Genus:** *Phantasma*
**Species:** *P. specularis*

## Physical Description

The Mirrorghast is a medium-sized spectral entity distinguished by its luminous, mirror-like surface that reflects ambient light in hypnotic patterns. The creature exists in a semi-corporeal state, constantly shifting between crystalline solidity and ethereal transparency. When illuminated, it displays an opalescent sheen with prismatic refractions, though observers note the light appears inverted. The entity measures approximately 1.5-2 metres in height and maintains a vaguely humanoid silhouette.

## Habitat

Mirrorghasts inhabit caves with reflective mineral deposits, underground lakes, and crystalline cavern systems where light scatters through natural prisms. They are most active during twilight hours when ambient light is dim. These spectral entities congregate in caves containing quartz formations and selenite deposits, suggesting sensitivity to mineral composition.

## Diet

The Mirrorghast sustains itself through the consumption of light itself, absorbing radiant energy from bioluminescent organisms and reflected sunlight. Evidence suggests they feed on concentrated ambient illumination rather than conventional prey, with feeding behaviors intensifying during dawn and dusk transitions.

## Behaviour

Mirrorghasts exhibit curious, investigative behaviour toward moving light sources, pursuing travelers who carry torches or lanterns. They display intelligence in their hunting patterns but show no apparent aggression unless startled. Most encounters conclude with the creature retreating deeper into darkness when threats materialize.

## Threat Level

**2 out of 5** — Mirrorghasts pose minimal direct threat to armed parties. Their attraction to light creates risk through luring travelers into hazardous terrain rather than through physical confrontation. Encounters remain unpredictable due to their ethereal nature and limited understanding of their intentions.
