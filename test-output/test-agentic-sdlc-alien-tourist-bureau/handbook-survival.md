## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: A human offered me "coffee." Is this an attack?**
A: No. Per Advisory GFBTT-HAZ-2026-001, coffee is a mild psychoactive stimulant offered as a social bonding gesture. Accept it. Declining carries unpredictable consequences. Maintain 3-meter distance from humans who have not yet consumed their daily dose.

**Q: A human asked "How are you?" and I provided a full physiological readout. They appeared distressed.**
A: Consult Guide GFBTT-CUL-2026-001. This is a social initialization sequence, not a diagnostic request. The approved response is "Good, thanks" regardless of actual condition. Honest answers caused distress in 94% of observed interactions.

**Q: I ordered "Eggs Benedict" and received neither eggs from anyone named Benedict nor an explanation.**
A: Per Guide GFBTT-CUL-2026-002, menu names bear no relationship to contents. "Hot Dog" contains no canine matter. "Chicken Fingers" references appendages the animal lacks. Point at what neighboring humans are consuming.

**Q: My limb count changed during the visit. Do I need paperwork?**
A: Yes. Per Form GFBTT-7G (GFBTT-ADM-2026-001), limb changes require Amendment Form GFBTT-7G-A. Report to Counter 7B. Do not speak to humans en route.

**Q: A human vehicle nearly struck me and I teleported reflexively. Humans are photographing the location.**
A: You have violated Advisory GFBTT-HAZ-2026-002: do not teleport near intersection cameras. Vacate immediately.

**Q: A human said "We should get together sometime." When should I arrive?**
A: Do not arrive. Per the Phrase Translation Table in Guide GFBTT-CUL-2026-001, this means "We will never meet again."

## Known Issues and Errata

1. **GFBTT-HAZ-2026-001:** The Risk Assessment Matrix rates coffee toxicity as LOW for carbon-based visitors. This does not apply to silicon-based species. Addendum forthcoming.

2. **GFBTT-HAZ-2026-002:** Tokyo's MODERATE rating (3.1/10) has been disputed by the Cetacean Diplomatic Delegation, who find pedestrian crossings disorienting.

3. **GFBTT-CUL-2026-002:** The tipping table lists Japan at 0%. Visitors who tipped generously "to compensate" caused offense. Zero means zero.

4. **GFBTT-ADM-2026-001:** The prohibited items list omits holoprojectors calibrated to simulate Terran domestic animals. This is permitted. The Bureau accepts no liability for resulting "viral videos."
