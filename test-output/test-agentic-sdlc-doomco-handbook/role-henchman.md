# Role Profile: Henchman (General)

**Department:** Operations & Minion Management

## Position Summary

The Henchman (General) serves as a foundational operational unit within Doomco's evil enterprise infrastructure. This role reports directly to regional supervisors and supports all Mad Scientist initiatives by executing assigned tasks with minimal initiative and maximum compliance. Henchmen maintain facility operations, transport classified materials, and provide physical security presence.

## Required Evil Skills

- Unquestioning obedience to senior staff directives
- Basic physical conditioning and manual labor capability
- Tolerance for dangerous working conditions and questionable safety protocols
- Proficiency with standard issue equipment and weaponry
- Minimal independent decision-making capacity

## Common Workplace Violations

- Questioning orders from supervisors (disciplinary record required)
- Attempting to understand evil corporate objectives
- Requesting adequate protective equipment or hazard compensation

## Evil Performance Metrics

- **Compliance Rate:** 98% or higher adherence to assigned tasks
- **Facility Uptime:** 99.5% operational availability during scheduled shifts
- **Equipment Accountability:** Zero unauthorized equipment loss per quarterly review
