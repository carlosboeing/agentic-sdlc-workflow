# Gliese-667c -- For the Adventurous Traveller

## Overview

Gliese-667c presents an unparalleled opportunity for the intrepid explorer seeking environments of considerable terrestrial challenge. The system's triple-star configuration creates a truly dynamic climate characterized by persistent thermal and radiative extremes. Visitors should prepare accordingly.

## Climate

Expect variable atmospheric conditions ranging from scorching solar flares to extended periods of cosmic radiation exposure. Temperature fluctuations exceed 200 degrees Celsius. The BITS recommends comprehensive shielding equipment and frequent atmospheric monitoring.

## Attractions

- Witness the spectacular triple-sunset phenomenon from specially reinforced observation platforms
- Explore crystalline formations created by extreme thermal cycling processes
- Document rare exotic mineral deposits found nowhere else in the galaxy

## Safety Rating

**4 out of 5** — Multiple concurrent environmental hazards present genuine mortality risk despite precautions.

## Bureau Advisory

The Bureau of Interstellar Tourism and Safety strongly advises comprehensive hazmat certification before departure. Travel insurance premiums average 50,000 Galactic Credits per person per day.
