**Date Filed:** 2026-03-14
**Filed By:** HR Department, FairyTale Corp
**Subject:** Cinderella, Events Coordinator

## Summary

A glass slipper was found blocking the fire exit on Floor 3, violating Building Safety Code Section 3.1. This is the third incident this quarter. Previous verbal warnings were issued on 2026-01-14 and 2026-02-22.

## Witness Statement

> "The slipper near the exit is more of a conversation piece than a genuine hazard, though it does technically obstruct passage."
> — Prince Charming, VP of Client Relations

## HR Recommendation

A formal written warning and mandatory Fire Safety Awareness module completion within five business days.
