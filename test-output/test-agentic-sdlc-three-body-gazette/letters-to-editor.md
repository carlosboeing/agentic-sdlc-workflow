# Letters to the Editor

## Three-Body Survival Gazette

---

Dear Editor,

On the morning of Stellar Date 7,441.09, I received a mandatory rehydration notice from the Bureau of Stellar Forecasting, declaring the Chaotic Era concluded. I complied immediately, enduring the standard 14-minute rehydration discomfort. At 7,441.09 plus 22 minutes, I received a correction notice: the Chaotic Era had not concluded. I was then required to re-dehydrate.

The physical consequences of this error are well-documented in Bureau literature. My fiber density has measurably decreased. I have filed Form 7-B (Premature Rehydration Claim) and request compensation of 2,400 credits for medical assessment and lost productivity. I will escalate to the Bureau Oversight Committee if no response is received within 10 stellar cycles.

— Huo-Ren, District 14

---

Dear Editor,

I am the overnight supervisor of Municipal Dehydration Warehouse 12, Sector 9. I write to report a recurring safety matter that management has declined to address.

Children have been entering the facility during off-hours and engaging in unsupervised play among the stacked dehydrated citizens. On three separate occasions I have observed the following: children using dehydrated adults as sleds down Stack Row C's inclined floor; deliberate rearrangement of stacks in Rows A through D, disrupting our sequential rehydration registry; and, most critically, the partial rehydration of one citizen's left leg when a child tampered with Rehydration Unit 4.

The partially rehydrated citizen was Ku-Yong, Row D, Stack 7. He has since been restored. Parental accountability and perimeter fencing are both required.

— Shen-Hua, Warehouse Supervisor, District 9

---

Dear Editor,

I have endured the gravitational tides from the recent binary syzygy of Sun-2 and Sun-3 with reasonable patience. That patience has concluded.

During peak syzygy alignment on Stellar Date 7,443.17, the structural groaning in my residential unit (Block 44, District 6) was sustained for nine hours. Three wall fixtures detached. My furniture migrated 0.4 meters toward the southern wall. When the Sun rose the following morning after syzygy release, I found my storage shelving collapsed entirely.

I formally request that the Stellar Council establish gravitational noise ordinances with measurable decibel-equivalent thresholds, and that the Bureau of Housing provide subsidized gravitational damper installation for pre-renovation units. This is a livability issue, not a theoretical one.

— Bai-Jing, District 6
