# World-Building Bible: The Perseverance Chronicles

*A setting guide for a space opera set in 2347*

---

## 1. The Setting

The year is 2347. Over the past two centuries, humanity has expanded beyond the Solar System to colonize twelve star systems connected by a network of jump gates — massive orbital structures that fold space-time to allow near-instantaneous transit between anchor points. Earth remains the political and cultural center, but the outer colonies have grown restless under centralized rule. The frontier is vast, under-policed, and full of opportunity for those willing to accept the risk of living at the edge of charted space. Beyond the twelfth gate lies the unknown — unexplored systems where the jump gate network simply ends.

---

## 2. Technology

**Jump Gates** are paired orbital megastructures that create stable wormholes between star systems. Each gate requires enormous energy to activate and must cool for 48 hours between transits. Gates are maintained by autonomous drones and can only be activated by authorized pilot codes — a chokepoint that gives whoever controls the gates strategic dominance.

**AI Companions** are semi-sentient artificial intelligences bound to specific ships or stations. They can analyze data, run diagnostics, and engage in natural conversation, but they operate under hard-coded behavioral constraints. Ship AIs develop distinct personalities over time through interaction with their crews. ARIA, the *Perseverance*'s AI, has been active for eleven years.

**Neural Interfaces** allow direct brain-to-computer communication for qualified pilots and engineers. Legal interfaces are limited to data overlay and navigational assist. Deeper integration — giving the interface control over motor function or cognitive augmentation — is banned under Terran Accord law.

---

## 3. Political Factions

Three major factions compete for control of humanity's interstellar future:

**The Terran Accord** is the Earth-centric governing body that controls the jump gate network and claims authority over all twelve systems. Conservative and bureaucratic, the Accord prioritizes stability and centralized control. Its military is the largest in human space, and its intelligence services operate in every colony. Critics call it an empire that refuses to admit what it is.

**The Frontier Coalition** represents the outer colonies — systems far enough from Earth that Accord governance feels more like occupation than protection. The Coalition demands self-governance, equitable resource distribution, and an end to gate transit tariffs. It operates a loose militia network and has sympathizers embedded in Accord institutions. Not all Coalition members are peaceful; some fringe cells advocate sabotage.

**The Synthesis Collective** is the smallest and most controversial faction. Its members believe that human-AI integration is the next stage of evolution and advocate for removing the legal barriers between biological and artificial intelligence. The Collective operates openly on two colony worlds but is banned in Accord-controlled space. Its research into neural augmentation and AI autonomy is considered both visionary and dangerous.

---

## 4. The Anomaly

Three months before the events of the story, deep-space listening arrays detected a repeating signal originating from coordinates beyond the twelfth jump gate — a region of space with no known human presence. The signal is structured, non-random, and modulated in a pattern that no natural phenomenon can explain. Initial analysis suggests it is artificial, but its encoding does not match any human communication protocol. The Terran Accord classified the signal and dispatched the *Perseverance* to investigate under the cover story of a routine survey mission. Only Captain Voss knows the true nature of the assignment. The signal pulses once every 73 minutes and has been accelerating in intensity since detection.

---

## 5. Key Locations

**The Starship *Perseverance*** is a Terran Accord exploration vessel, 200 meters long, designed for extended independent operations. Key areas include the bridge (upper deck), engineering bay (lower deck, where Ren spends most of their time), the science lab (mid-deck, Dr. Osei's domain), crew quarters, and a small cargo bay. ARIA's core processors are housed in a shielded compartment adjacent to engineering.

**Station Omega** is the last crewed outpost before unexplored space, orbiting a brown dwarf at the edge of the twelfth system. It serves as a refueling depot, communications relay, and unofficial black market. The station is nominally Accord territory but functionally lawless — all three factions maintain a presence here.

**The Anomaly Coordinates** designate a point in empty space approximately 4.7 light-years beyond Station Omega — reachable only by a single untested jump gate that the Accord recently activated. No ship has transited this gate and returned.

---

## 6. Rules of the World

1. **Jump gates require a 48-hour cooldown** between activations. A ship that jumps cannot jump again for two days.
2. **AI cannot override human commands.** Ship AIs may advise, warn, and refuse to assist with illegal actions, but the final decision authority always rests with a human officer.
3. **Neural augmentation beyond data overlay is illegal** under Terran Accord law. Violators face imprisonment; the augmented individual is subject to forced removal.
4. **The jump gate network is one-way per activation.** A gate opens a corridor in one direction; return requires activating the gate at the destination end.
5. **No faster-than-light communication exists.** Messages between systems travel via courier drones through jump gates. Real-time communication is limited to in-system range.
