# Travel Advisory: The Substance Known as "Coffee"

**Classification:** UNCLASSIFIED -- FOR TOURIST USE
**Document No.:** GFBTT-HAZ-2026-001
**Issuing Authority:** Department of Hazardous Consumables, Galactic Federation Bureau of Terran Tourism
**Effective Date:** Galactic Standard Date 7741.22 (Terran: March 15, 2026)
**Distribution:** All registered Terran visitors
**Supersedes:** None

---

## 1. Overview

This advisory concerns a thermally activated plant extract ("coffee") produced from roasted *Coffea* seeds. Approximately 2.25 billion humans consume it daily. The Bureau classifies it as a **mild psychoactive stimulant**. Visitors are reminded: coffee is not a beverage. It is a dependency management system.

## 2. Observed Behavioral States

- **Pre-consumption:** Reduced cognition, elevated irritability. Locally designated "before coffee." Do not initiate contact.
- **Post-consumption (30+ min):** Normal interaction resumes. Safe to approach.

Withdrawal manifests within 24 hours. Do not comment on this.

## 3. Risk Assessment Matrix

| Category | Rating | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Toxicity** (common visitor species) | LOW | Harmless to most carbon-based visitors |
| **Social Hazard Level** | HIGH | Interrupting morning ritual may be interpreted as aggression |
| **Addiction Potential** | SEVERE | Humans do not acknowledge this; do not raise it |
| **Recommended Visitor Response** | OBSERVE ONLY | Accept if offered; decline at own risk |

## 4. Visitor Protocols

- Maintain 3-meter distance from un-dosed humans.
- Do not suggest alternatives. This has been documented as a provocation.
- If offered coffee, accept. Declining carries unpredictable social consequences.
- The dispensing establishments ("cafes") may be entered freely.
