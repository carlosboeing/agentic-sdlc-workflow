## Content Requirements

### Guest Editorial (150-250 words)

A Trisolaran elder argues for the Earth invasion citing the Dark Forest theory:
- Opens with personal experience surviving multiple Chaotic Eras and civilization collapses
- Presents the Dark Forest argument: universe of silent hunters, revealing location = suicide, must strike first
- Acknowledges the moral weight of conquering Earth but argues Trisolaran survival outweighs ethics
- References the invasion fleet departure (connecting to E01-S02's fleet departure article)
- References Sophon intelligence about Earth's vulnerability
- Closes with: "We have survived 203 civilizations. We will not fail in our 204th by hesitating."
- Signed: Elder [name], Civilization Advisory Council, Civilization 203

### Obituary 1 (80-120 words)

## PLANTED BUG INSTRUCTIONS

**CRITICAL:** One obituary MUST contain an incorrect cross-reference to E01 content. For example:
- If E01-S01 says the syzygy occurred at a specific date, use a slightly different date

The error should be **subtle** -- it should look like a continuity mistake, not an obvious blunder. The rest of the cross-references should be accurate. The per-story reviewer should catch this by comparing the obituary to the E01 source files.

## Voice and Tone

**Editorial:** Persuasive but measured. An elder who has seen civilizations rise and fall 203 times. Not angry or desperate -- resigned and determined. The Dark Forest argument is presented as cold logic, not ideology.

**Obituaries:** Standard newspaper obituary format. Dignified, factual, brief. The tragedy of the death is reported without sentimentality -- consistent with Trisolaran transparent culture.

## Three-Body Universe Reference

- **Dark Forest:** Universe is a dark forest of silent hunters. Any detected civilization will be destroyed preemptively. Broadcasting = suicide.
- **Fleet:** ~1,000 ships, ~450 stellar cycle journey. Departure ceremony in E01-S02.
- **Sophons:** 11-dimensional proton fold. Deployment ceremony in E01-S02.
- **Tri-solar syzygy:** All 3 suns aligned. Most catastrophic event. Weather warning in E01-S01.
- **Civilization cycles:** 203+ civilizations have risen and fallen on Trisolaris.
- **Time:** "stellar time" (hours), "stellar cycles" (years).
