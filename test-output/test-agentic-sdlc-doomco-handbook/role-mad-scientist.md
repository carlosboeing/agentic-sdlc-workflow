# Role Profile: Mad Scientist

**Department:** Research & Doomsday Development

## Position Summary

The Mad Scientist develops and maintains our strategic doomsday initiatives with minimal regard for conventional physics or ethics. This role requires enthusiasm for chaos, synthetic DNA modification, and interdimensional portal prototyping. Successful candidates have demonstrated mastery of lab safety protocols that are intentionally circumvented.

## Required Evil Skills

- Advanced explosives engineering (particularly with unorthodox fuel sources)
- Proficiency in creating sentient life forms without proper containment
- Exceptional organizational skills for hoarding scientific equipment
- Ability to cackle maniacally while conducting peer review
- Fluent in at least three dead languages

## Common Workplace Violations

- Using company isotopes for personal weather manipulation experiments
- Neglecting to label caustic solutions with skull and crossbones
- Operating unauthorized cloning chambers in the break room

## Evil Performance Metrics

- Doomsday Device Functionality Index: 85% operational success rate
- Laboratory Incident Reduction Target: No more than 12 explosions quarterly
- Evil Science Publication Volume: 4 peer-reviewed papers in questionable journals
