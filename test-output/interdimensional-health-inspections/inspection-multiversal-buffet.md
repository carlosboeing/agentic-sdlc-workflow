## Executive Summary

The Multiversal Buffet is the only establishment licensed to serve cuisine from every known dimension simultaneously. Its kitchen spans 47 dimensions, and its dining room occupies a pocket dimension engineered to contain all possible table configurations. This annual review assessed compliance across all MFSA regulatory domains, drawing upon five inspections conducted during the current cycle. The establishment is rated **Conditionally Compliant**, with corrective actions required in three categories.

---

## Findings

The Buffet's Dimension 4-Alpha station exhibits the quantum superposition food handling practices cited in MFSA-INS-2026-001 (The Quantum Bistro). Proteins at the Alpha wing carving station persist in unresolved cooked/raw superposition until a patron's fork collapses the wavefunction. Unlike the Quantum Bistro, however, the Buffet has deployed Observer Protocol 7 at each serving tray, ensuring state resolution prior to plating. This Authority considers the mitigation adequate.

The Dimension N noodle bar sources its product from the same supplier as the establishment cited in MFSA-INS-2026-003 (The Nth Dimension Noodle House). During inspection, two noodle filaments breached the N/N+1 boundary and extended into the adjacent dessert station. The Buffet's containment barriers are rated to N+3, exceeding the minimum standard, but the filament breach indicates calibration drift. Recalibration has been ordered.

The Void-sourced tasting menu warrants scrutiny following the emergency shutdown documented in MFSA-INS-2026-005 (The Void Kitchen). The Buffet sources Void ingredients through a controlled conceptual extraction aperture rather than the unregulated "reality unweaving" method that caused patron cessation events at The Void Kitchen. Patron continuity monitoring is active and no existence interruptions have been recorded. Quarterly audits are mandated.

The Dimension 7-Gamma probability dessert cart operates under the same framework inspected in MFSA-INS-2026-004 (The Probability Cafe). Neutron-star containment rings are present, and the Buffet maintains a dedicated gravitational incident response team on retainer. Retroactive non-existence logs are filed per MFSA Form 7G-REX.

## Comparative Analysis

Against peer establishments inspected this cycle, the Multiversal Buffet demonstrates materially stronger compliance. The Quantum Bistro (MFSA-INS-2026-001) received failing grades in Food Temperature Compliance and Operating Hours Compliance, areas where the Buffet maintains B or higher ratings across all stations. The Nth Dimension Noodle House (MFSA-INS-2026-003) continues to struggle with dimensional containment and portion-size invariance, both of which the Buffet manages through adaptive barriers and absolute-baseline portioning. Unlike either comparator, the Buffet has never received a CRITICAL or EMERGENCY classification.
