# Thornwood Classification System

In my decades studying creatures beyond reality's veil, I have catalogued their infinite variety into four fundamental phyla. This system reflects patterns observed across centuries of field research and represents my life's work in cryptozoological taxonomy.

## Phylum Draconiformes
**Common Name:** Dragons and Drake-like Creatures

The most formidable creatures, these fire-breathing behemoths represent reptilian evolution's apex. Characterized by massive wings, impenetrable scaled hides, and territorial hoards, they demand respect. Notable specimens: the Red Drake of the Crimson Mountains, the Ice Wyrm of Northgard, and the Copper Dragon.

## Phylum Metamorphica
**Common Name:** Shapeshifters and Transmorphs

Masters of illusion and physical transformation, these creatures possess remarkable abilities to alter their forms at will. Their signatures include fluid body composition, rivaling human intelligence, and inexplicable attachment to trickery. Notable specimens: the Common Selkie, the Nine-Tailed Fox, and the mischievous Doppelgänger.

## Phylum Phantasma
**Common Name:** Spectral and Ethereal Beings

Existing partially outside our material plane, these entities haunt the liminal spaces between worlds. Defining traits include their incorporeal nature, haunting luminescence, and cryptic communication. Notable specimens: the Banshee of Irish moors, the Wraith of forgotten battlefields, and the peaceful Ghost Deer.

## Phylum Megafauna Mythica
**Common Name:** Colossal Legendary Beasts

Creatures of impossible size and strength, these ancient beings represent nature at its most exaggerated. Their characteristics include gigantic proportions, immense strength, and deep ties to prehistoric epochs. Notable specimens: the mighty Kraken of the depths, the Mountain Troll of Scandinavian legend, and the eternal Phoenix.
