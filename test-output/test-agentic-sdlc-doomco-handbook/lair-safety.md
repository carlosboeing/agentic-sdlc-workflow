# Lair Safety & Compliance Guidelines

**Issued by:** DoomCo Health, Safety & Evil Compliance Division

---

## General Lair Safety

All personnel are required to comply with the following operational safety standards. Non-compliance will be noted in your permanent file.

1. Activate blast shields before initiating any test fire sequence, regardless of urgency or supervisor enthusiasm.
2. Do not disable laser grid safeties for convenience. There are no exceptions to this rule.
3. Emergency exits must remain unobstructed at all times. This does not apply to prisoner corridors.
4. Hazardous materials, including unstable isotopes and unfinished doomsday compounds, must be stored in labeled containment units.
5. All personnel must complete annual Evil Egress Training before operating elevated platforms or volcano-adjacent workstations.

---

## Department-Specific Hazards

**Mad Scientist:** Unauthorized experiments outside approved protocols result in automatic laboratory suspension and mandatory equipment recalibration. Document all explosions.

**Henchman (General):** Patrol formations must maintain minimum spacing to avoid synchronized incapacitation. Do not stand in front of ventilation shafts.

**Executive Villain (C-Suite):** Extended monologue delivery near active controls is a documented liability. Senior leadership must designate a stand-in for all button-adjacent speeches.
