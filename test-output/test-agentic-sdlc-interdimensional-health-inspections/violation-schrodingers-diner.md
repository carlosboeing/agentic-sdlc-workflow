## Order to Cease and Desist

Pursuant to inspection report MFSA-INS-2026-002, this Authority hereby issues a formal Cease and Desist Order to Schrodinger's Diner. The establishment is directed to immediately halt all operations presenting unresolvable quantum compliance paradoxes.

## Practices That Must Cease Immediately

1. Service of "The Cat's Meow Casserole" in any superposition of edible and non-edible states
2. Maintenance of temperature logs in simultaneous compliant and non-compliant states (MFSA-VIO-4003)
3. Display of health permits existing concurrently in valid and invalid states (MFSA-VIO-4004)
4. Operation of kitchen facilities whose hygiene can only be determined post-collapse (MFSA-VIO-4001)

## Legal Basis

This order is issued under **MFSA Section 12.4** (prohibition on indeterminate food safety states), **MFSA Section 7.1(b)** (mandatory verifiability of compliance records), and **MFSA Regulation 9-Q** (Quantum Uncertainty Exemption procedures — no application on file).

## Conditions for Resumption of Operations

1. Filing of a variance application under MFSA Regulation 9-Q
2. Collapse of kitchen hygiene into a single deterministic compliant state
3. Removal of "The Cat's Meow Casserole" from the active menu pending verification

This Authority acknowledges the philosophical difficulty of verifying compliance when observation constitutes interference. That difficulty does not constitute an exemption.
