## Executive Summary

Field surveys confirm the single-occupancy phone booth has reached critically endangered status within the open-plan biome. The structure is occupied an average of 23.7 hours per day by specimens of *Homo corporatus* (ref: HC-CLASS-001) who uniformly claim they "just need five minutes," a vocalisation bearing no correlation to actual duration.

## Threat Analysis

The primary threat is territorial squatting — classified as *occupatio perpetua* — whereby a specimen claims the booth at 09:02 and emerges only when the cleaning crew arrives. Secondary degradation stems from unofficial repurposing as a crying room following mass social distress events (ref: S2, "The Reply-All Incident"). Management has proposed converting all surviving booths into "collaboration pods," eliminating the habitat entirely while adding beanbags. The subspecies *H. corporatus managerus* (HC-CLASS-001) drives this initiative.

## Conservation Recommendation

This office recommends immediate protected status under the Open Office Endangered Habitats Act. Enforcement shall include mandatory fifteen-minute occupancy limits administered via a passive-aggressive countdown timer visible to the entire floor, accompanied by escalating sighs from the waiting queue (ref: S2, "The Thermostat Wars").
