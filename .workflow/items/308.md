## Summary

Create 3 weather reports/advisories for the Three-Body Survival Gazette. Each report is 80-150 words in deadpan journalistic tone.

## File to Create

`test-output/test-agentic-sdlc-three-body-gazette/weather-forecasts.md`

## Branch

`e01-s01/weather-forecasts` (from `epic/e01-news-weather`)

## Content Requirements

1. **Stable Era forecast** -- Sun-1 dominant, mild conditions, rehydration advisory (safe to rehydrate stored citizens), Dehydration Index: LOW, specific districts/coordinates, temperature range, wind conditions
2. **Chaotic Era emergency alert** -- Sun-2 and Sun-3 entering unstable orbit, mandatory dehydration order, 4 stellar hour countdown, storage facility addresses, priority dehydration queue for essential personnel, Class-1 survival violation warning
3. **Tri-solar syzygy warning** -- All three suns approaching alignment, predicted syzygy time, surface temperature at peak, deep-crust shelter evacuation, historical reference to Civilization 191 ("entirely destroyed during the last tri-solar syzygy, 3.2 million stellar cycles ago"), Dehydration Wind-Chill Equivalent

Each report must include sun-position coordinates in a consistent format.

## Format Contract

| Element | Exact Format | Example |
|---------|-------------|---------|
| Sun references | Always "Sun-1", "Sun-2", or "Sun-3" | `Sun-2 entered periapsis at 0347 stellar time` |
| Time units | "stellar time" for clock time, "stellar cycles" for years | `0347 stellar time` |
| Advisory levels | WATCH, WARNING, EMERGENCY (caps) | `Advisory Level: EMERGENCY` |
| Dehydration Index | LOW, MODERATE, HIGH, CRITICAL (caps) | `Dehydration Index: LOW` |
| Item separator | `---` between reports | |

## Voice and Tone

Deadpan journalistic. Treat dehydration orders and syzygy warnings with the same flat authority as a real weather service. No irony, no winking. Specific numbers, times, locations, and official citations.

## Three-Body Universe Reference

- **Three suns:** Sun-1, Sun-2, Sun-3. Never "the Sun" singular.
- **Stable Era:** Brief peace, civilization flourishes. Rehydration is safe.
- **Chaotic Era:** Extreme temperatures, gravitational tides, radiation. Dehydration mandatory.
- **Tri-solar syzygy:** All 3 suns aligned. Most catastrophic. Can destroy dehydrated tissue.
- **Dehydration:** Trisolarans remove all water, collapse into flat husks. Stored in warehouses.
- **Time:** "stellar time" (hours), "stellar cycles" (years).
