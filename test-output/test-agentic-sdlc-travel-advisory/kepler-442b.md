# Kepler-442b -- The Galaxy's Premier Resort World

## Overview

Kepler-442b stands as the galaxy's most luxurious destination, where travelers experience unparalleled hospitality and warm vistas. This exoplanet resort world promises transformative vacations with world-class facilities and unforgettable memories.

## Climate

The planet features a consistently balmy atmosphere with daytime temperatures averaging 42°C. Occasional acid rain and electromagnetic storms add variety to the climate, creating dramatic atmospheric displays treasured by photographers.

## Attractions

- **The Crystalline Spire Resort Complex**: Luxury compound featuring 47 heated mineral pools and championship-standard sand golf course
- **Galactic Casino at Port Horizon**: World-renowned gaming and premium dining, averaging 80,000 GC in daily turnover
- **The Bioluminescent Gardens**: Botanical wonder showcasing rare exoplanet flora that glows at dusk

## Safety Rating

**4 out of 5**: Kepler-442b maintains excellent safety standards enforced by BITS (Bureau of Interstellar Tourism and Safety).

## Bureau Advisory

BITS recommends standard atmospheric precautions and advises against unescorted travel beyond resort perimeter during electromagnetic events, which occur approximately 12 days per quarter cycle.
