## Executive Summary

Field observation of *Homo corporatus*, including territorial behaviour at the communal coffee station (ref: Episode — "The Coffee Machine"), confirms a species of remarkable resilience yet narrow ecological range. The Species Classification Report (HC-CLASS-001) catalogues three subspecies occupying interdependent niches within the open-plan biome.

## Options Analysis

**(a) Full Protection under the Endangered Habitats Act.**
The species shows no risk of population decline. Shielding *H. corporatus* from natural pressures such as restructuring events would inhibit adaptive behaviour and compromise research integrity.

**(b) Controlled Observation with Minimal Intervention.**
Current protocols preserve the population's behavioural repertoire while permitting longitudinal study. This safeguards data quality without introducing observer effects.

**(c) Designation as a Cautionary Exhibit.**
The species' ritualistic inefficiencies offer unparalleled pedagogical value. Public exhibition would warn other species considering hierarchical social organisation.

## Formal Recommendation

This office recommends **Option (c): Cautionary Exhibit Designation**. The behavioural patterns in HC-CLASS-001 — particularly threat displays and territorial rituals — represent an invaluable case study. Designation permits continued observation while maximising this species' scientific contribution.
