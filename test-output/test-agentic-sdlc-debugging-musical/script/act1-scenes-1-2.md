# Act I, Scene 1: The Pitch

*A startup pitch competition. A podium. Upstage: a projection screen. Three INVESTORS sit at a judge's table.*

**Characters present:** Jordan Chase, Investors (ensemble)

---

*JORDAN CHASE bounds onstage in a blazer over a hoodie. The first slide reads: "CTRLZ: 10x The Undo."*

**JORDAN:** *(gesturing at the screen)* Friends. What if I told you — the undo button was broken?

*Slide advances: "Ctrl+Z as a Service™". Jordan breaks into song.*

---

### Song: "The Pitch"

**Performed by:** Jordan Chase (solo)
**Style:** Up-tempo show opener, jazz hands energy
**Key:** E major
**Tempo:** 160 BPM (Allegro vivace)

**[VERSE 1]**
We've got a vision and a deck with fifty slides,
A TAM of billions and a product no one's tried,
The market's broken and the undo button's dead —
We'll fix it with a pivot and a dream inside my head.

**[CHORUS]**
Ctrl+Z, Ctrl+Z, undo your worst mistakes,
Ctrl+Z, Ctrl+Z, whatever it takes,
Ship it live and fix it later, that's our creed —
Ctrl+Z is all the product that you'll ever need!

**[BRIDGE]**
*(Jordan climbs onto the judge's table)*
We're not selling software — we're selling second chances!
Disrupting the regret economy with interpretive dances!

**[FINAL CHORUS / TAG]**
Ctrl+Z! Ctrl+Z! Undo your worst mistakes!
The future's just a rollback, and the rollback's what we make!

*The INVESTORS exchange bewildered glances. One slowly begins to clap.*

# Act I, Scene 2: The Standup

*The next morning. CtrlZ open-plan office. Standing desks, a whiteboard drowning in sticky notes.*

**Characters present:** Marcus Chen, Zoe Kim, Priya Mehta, Aisha Okafor

---

**MARCUS:** *(staring at his laptop)* Main branch is broken.

**ZOE:** *(quietly)* I know.

**PRIYA:** *(opening her laptop)* The PRD covers this. If anyone had read it.

*A beat. Then, as one, they begin to sing.*

---

### Song: "The Standup Ballad"

**Performed by:** Full cast
**Style:** Power ballad, builds to ensemble
**Key:** D minor
**Tempo:** 72 BPM (Andante, building to Maestoso)

**[VERSE 1 — Marcus, solo]**
The pipeline is red and the logs don't lie —
The main branch is broken and I don't know why.

**[VERSE 2 — Zoe, solo]**
I've been rebasing for three days straight,
Every commit I touch becomes a war,
I don't remember what I'm rebasing for.

**[CHORUS — ensemble]**
Update your ticket, move the card to Done,
But nothing is done and the build never runs,
We stand in a circle and we sing of our pain —
The standup's not comedy, we're just going insane.

**[BRIDGE — Priya]**
I wrote the PRD in March, forty-seven pages long,
Every edge case, every arch —
No one read it. I was wrong.

**[FINAL CHORUS / TAG — full ensemble, fortissimo]**
Update your ticket! Move the card to Done!
The sprint ends on Friday but the sprint can't be won!
The standup's not comedy —
But we show up all the same.

*The team holds their positions. A Slack notification pings. Nobody moves.*
