## Summary

Terran ground-based transit -- colloquially termed "traffic" -- is a mass coordinated near-death experience in which millions of humans propel metal chassis at lethal velocities, separated from oncoming fatality by a painted line.

The behavioral anomaly known as "road rage" (a transient psychotic episode triggered by minor deviations from an unwritten social code) is endemic. Traffic signals -- a three-state color-based control mechanism -- are obeyed with greater compliance than most legislative instruments.

**Visitor Protocol:** Do not fly over traffic visibly. Do not teleport near intersection cameras. If operating a human vehicle: the left pedal halts; the right pedal accelerates. Reversing these is a terminal misconfiguration. Do not correct a human's driving.
