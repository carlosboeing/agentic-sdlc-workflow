# The Comprehensive Supernatural Pet Nutrition Guide

*Eternally Yours Supernatural Pet Adoption Agency — Certified Supernatural Animal Husbandry (SAH) Nutrition Team*

---

Feeding a supernatural pet is precise work. Most supernatural species cannot tolerate substitutions: a phoenix fed cold food will experience flame suppression, a selkie offered previously frozen fish may refuse meals for days, and a juvenile leviathan given void essence in a standard dropper risks a corrosive incident. This guide covers dietary requirements across three supernatural pet categories: meal planning, common mistakes, and allergen guidance.

---

## Fire-Elemental Nutrition

Fire-elemental pets process mineral-dense, high-temperature food. Never serve below 200°C — cold food causes digestive distress, lethargy, and in severe cases, temporary flame suppression.

**Cinder** (our phoenix) receives volcanic mineral supplements mixed into high-calcium charcoal pellets, served twice daily at a minimum of 200°C. The mineral blend supports his rebirth cycle and plumage health. Cinnamon bark treats are optional but behaviorally important.

**Flicker** (our fire salamander) eats live fire beetles and fresh ember moss, supplemented weekly with a sulfur crystal tablet. Ember moss must be fresh — dried moss loses the volatile compounds salamanders require for skin hydration. Flicker's basking surface must be maintained at 120°C; fire salamanders digest most efficiently when core temperature is elevated during and after meals.

**Spark** (our ember sprite) does not eat solid food. Sprites feed by absorbing heat directly from open flames — a gas fireplace, glass-surround candle station, or the Eternally Yours Sprite Feeding Hearth. A pinch of charcoal dust added to the flame twice daily provides essential mineral micronutrients. Never offer Spark solid food; it cannot be metabolized.

## Aquatic-Supernatural Nutrition

**Puddles** (our kraken hatchling) requires three meals daily: raw herring and mackerel morning and evening, live crustaceans at midday. The live midday feeding is enrichment as well as nutrition — a static diet suppresses hunt instincts and leads to behavioral regression. Twice weekly, deep-sea bioluminescent plankton supplement must be added to drinking water; partner vendor sourcing is required, as commercial substitutes lack the correct spectral signature for tentacle development.

**Marina** (our selkie pup) requires fresh salmon and cod twice daily — previously frozen fish risks disrupting her pelt recovery. Dried seaweed crumbled over each meal provides iodine and trace minerals. Three times weekly, Marina must receive moonlight-infused water: seawater left in direct natural moonlight for a minimum of four hours. Artificial light does not replicate the photonic compounds that support selkie pelt health. Marina is lactose intolerant; no dairy supplements of any kind.

**Abby** (our juvenile deep-sea leviathan) eats exclusively in darkness — all lights, including equipment indicators, must be off during every feeding. Her primary diet is deep-sea bioluminescent plankton from approved vendors, supplemented twice weekly with whole raw deep-water fish. Two drops of void essence per 100 liters of tank water must be added weekly using the provided stainless steel dropper only; void essence corrodes standard glass.

## Interdimensional Pet Nutrition

**Void essence** is required by several interdimensional species. Store in sealed amber glass away from direct light; improper storage renders it inert within 48 hours. Use stainless steel or borosilicate glass only — void essence corrodes standard metals. Never microwave it; electromagnetic fields cause phase-shift and localized dimensional instability.

**Temporal crumbs** — crystallized fragments of temporal residue — are supplemental treats for temporally sensitive species. Their shelf life is unpredictable: a package may expire before opening, or well after its stamped date. Store in lead-lined containers.

**Phase-stable treats** are formulated for pets that phase through matter. Standard treats pass through phasing animals without being consumed; phase-stable treats contain dimensional anchoring compounds that persist through the phase state. Overfeeding causes mild temporal indigestion.

## Common Dietary Mistakes

- **Do NOT** serve fire-elemental food below 200°C. Cold food causes flame suppression in phoenixes and metabolic slowdown in salamanders.

- **Do NOT** offer Spark solid food. Ember sprites are obligate flame-feeders; solid food is not metabolized.

- **Do NOT** use previously frozen fish for Marina. Selkies detect protein degradation with high sensitivity.

- **Do NOT** add moonlight-infused water during a lunar eclipse. The inverted photonic profile reverses its therapeutic benefit and may cause temporary form instability in selkies.

- **Do NOT** store void essence in standard glass, plastic, or metal containers. Sealed amber glass only.

- **Do NOT** microwave void essence. Microwave radiation causes phase-shift and localized dimensional instability.

- **Do NOT** feed Abby with the lights on. Abyssal-adapted photoreceptors mean any light during feeding causes appetite suppression.

- **Do NOT** substitute off-brand bioluminescent plankton for partner vendor product. The spectral signature differs and will not support Puddles' tentacle development.

## Allergen and Sensitivity Warnings

Supernatural pets present cross-dimensional allergen profiles that differ substantially from conventional animals.

**Sulfur crystals** — essential for Flicker — are contraindicated for all aquatic supernatural species. Trace sulfur exposure disrupts Marina's pelt chemistry and causes respiratory distress in Puddles. Keep fire-elemental and aquatic-supernatural feeding areas physically separated.

**Moonlight-infused water** must never be offered to fire-elemental pets. The photonic compounds that benefit selkies suppress the heat emission fire-elementals require for thermoregulation.

**Void essence** should not be introduced near fire-elemental feeding areas; its phase-shift properties interact unpredictably with high-temperature metabolic environments.

**Identifying food sensitivities in supernatural pets** requires species-specific observation:

- *Bioluminescence changes:* Unscheduled pulses in Abby or unusual glow shifts in Puddles often signal dietary irritation.
- *Involuntary phasing:* Unexpected phasing through solid objects may indicate a reaction to an incompatible food additive.
- *Localized temporal distortion around the food bowl:* Food aging faster or slower than expected — fresh fish spoiling in minutes, or remaining preserved past its window — is a serious sign. Discontinue that food source immediately and call the hotline.

Do not attempt to diagnose or treat dietary reactions independently. Certified SAH nutritionists are available around the clock on the support hotline.
