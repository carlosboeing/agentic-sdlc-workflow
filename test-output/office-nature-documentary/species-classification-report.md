## Researcher Notes

*H. corporatus* exhibits remarkable behavioural plasticity across subspecies while maintaining strong intraspecific hierarchies mediated by meeting room access and monitor count. Field observation confirms that *managerus* rarely produces artefacts independently, yet claims territorial dominion over all deliverables. *Internus* specimens display acute threat sensitivity and are observed mirroring dominant individuals as a survival strategy.
