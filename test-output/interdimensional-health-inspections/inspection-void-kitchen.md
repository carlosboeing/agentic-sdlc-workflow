## Executive Summary

Following a Priority-0 complaint, this Authority dispatched Inspector Mun after three patrons ceased to exist mid-meal. The Void Kitchen was located by triangulating the absence of a fixed address. The establishment sources ingredients by extracting them from the fabric of reality, a practice this Authority has provisionally classified as "probably a health code violation."

## Shutdown Order

Pursuant to the **Multiversal Food Safety Act**, the following statutes are cited:

- **MFSA § 88.4 (Ingredient Sourcing):** No establishment may source ingredients by dismantling the physical fabric of any dimension. "Unweaving concepts" is prohibited regardless of flavour outcome.
- **MFSA § 112.1 (Patron Continuity):** Establishments must ensure patrons remain in existence for the duration of dining, including dessert.
- **MFSA § 201.7 (Fixed Premises Requirement):** "The space between spaces, but warmer" does not constitute a locatable address.

**The Void Kitchen is hereby CLOSED.** Operating licence ∅ is suspended pending ontological review, demonstration of a fixed location, and completion of the MFSA Patron Retention Certification course.
