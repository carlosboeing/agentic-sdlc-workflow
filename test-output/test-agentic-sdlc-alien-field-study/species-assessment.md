| Intelligence Rating | 4.2 / 10 (disputed)[^1] |
| Threat Level | Low |
| Locomotion | Bipedal; structurally unstable at speed |
| Communication | Sound-based; single-channel; demonstrably inefficient |

---

## Recommended Contact Protocol

First contact, if deemed necessary, should be conducted via non-luminous vessel at altitudes exceeding 12,000 metres to minimise observational contamination. Field agents are advised to limit exposure to no more than four local minutes, as the species demonstrates a compulsive tendency to document encounters. Direct communication is not recommended pending resolution of the intelligence rating dispute.

[^1]: Contested in *Journal of Questionable Fauna*, Vol. 14, Issue 2 — dissenting authors argue the rating reflects instrument miscalibration rather than biological capacity.
