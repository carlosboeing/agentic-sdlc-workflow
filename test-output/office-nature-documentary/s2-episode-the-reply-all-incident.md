# Episode 2: The Reply-All Incident

The specimen emerges from its den—a pale cubicle of synthetic fabric and glowing screens. It moves with purpose, clicking the mouse. But something shifts. A notification arrives in the communication pool. The specimen, possessed by curiosity, depresses the reply-all key rather than the singular reply mechanism. In one catastrophic moment, what should have been a private missive broadcasts across the entire colony, 847 recipients now frozen in horrified witness. [CAMERA: pulls back slowly to show rows of paralyzed office workers, faces blank with shock] The collective inhalation—sharp, synchronized, audible—marks the onset of *silentium mortis*, the death-quiet that descends upon social catastrophe. Shoulders hunch. Eyes dart. The specimen's breathing becomes shallow. Recognition blooms: the error is irreversible. Without word, without gesture, the male specimen abandons its workstation and retreats toward the restroom refuge habitat, the primal retreat of the deeply mortified. [SFX: gentle footsteps receding down hallway]
