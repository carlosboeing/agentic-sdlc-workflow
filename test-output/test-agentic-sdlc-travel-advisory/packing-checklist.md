# BITS Official Packing Checklist for Interstellar Travel

*Bureau of Interstellar Tourism and Safety.*

| Item | Quantity | Destination(s) | Notes |
|------|----------|----------------|-------|
| Atmospheric filter mask | 2 | Gliese-667c, Proxima Station | Gliese-667c requires the Mk-IV Deluxe; standard-grade suffices for Proxima Station. Upgrade costs are not covered. |
| Radiation sunscreen (SPF 9000) | 1 tube | Kepler-442b, Gliese-667c | Resort staff at Kepler-442b sell a branded version for 400 Galactic Credits (GC). Pack your own. |
| Emergency beacon | 1 | Gliese-667c | Required under BITS Regulation 7.4. Statistically unlikely to help, but reassures next of kin. |
| Currency adapter card | 1 | Proxima Station | Pre-load GC before arrival. Exchange rates change hourly and apparently at random. |
| Thermal underlayer | 3 | Kepler-442b, Gliese-667c | Removing layers at Kepler-442b beach resorts is subject to local ordinances. |
| Diplomatic waiver (BITS-7B) | 2 copies | Kepler-442b, Gliese-667c, Proxima Station | One copy for officials; one for when officials misplace the first. |

---

*BITS provides this checklist as a public service. Items listed do not guarantee safe passage or continued biological function. Consult Advisory Bulletin 44-C before departure.*
