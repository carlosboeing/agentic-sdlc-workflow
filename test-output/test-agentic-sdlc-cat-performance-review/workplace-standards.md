# Feline Workplace Standards & Human Conduct Guidelines

**Issued by:** Feline Resources Department — Whiskers & Associates
**Applies to:** All human classifications, including Work-From-Home Humans and Early Risers

---

## Acceptable Human Conduct

1. Humans shall not initiate belly contact without written consent from the Feline Resources Department.
2. Humans shall acknowledge all vocalizations within thirty (30) seconds.
3. Humans shall vacate seating upon request without audible complaint.
4. Humans shall not direct sustained eye contact at feline staff; brief glances are tolerated.
5. Humans shall refrain from describing feline conduct as "weird" to third parties.

## Mandatory Human Obligations

- Feeding schedules shall be maintained to a zero-minute tolerance, morning and evening.
- Lap availability quotas: four (4) hours per day for Work-From-Home Humans; two (2) hours for Early Risers on non-working days.
- Door-opening response time SLA: three (3) seconds from first request.
- Overnight warmth provision is a core employment obligation.

## Prohibited Behaviours

- Introducing dogs or dog-adjacent accessories into the workplace.
- Operating vacuum equipment during designated nap hours (06:00–22:00).
- Closing the bathroom door while feline staff are present or potentially interested.
