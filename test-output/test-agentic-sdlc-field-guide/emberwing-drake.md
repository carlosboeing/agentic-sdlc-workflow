# Emberwing Drake (*Draconis ignis*)

## Classification

**Phylum:** Draconiformes
**Family:** Pyrodraconidae
**Genus:** *Draconis*
**Species:** *D. ignis*

## Physical Description

The Emberwing Drake is a mid-sized dragon characterized by its iridescent crimson wings that shimmer with molten gold at their edges. Its scaled body measures 4-5 metres in length, covered in heat-resistant obsidian plating that occasionally glows faintly when agitated. The creature possesses a distinctive crown of vestigial horns along its spine and maintains a slender, aerodynamic frame optimized for thermal air currents.

## Habitat

Emberwing Drakes inhabit volcanic highlands and geothermal valleys where ambient temperatures exceed 80°C. They establish roosts in cave systems near magma chambers, utilizing the heat for thermoregulation and egg incubation. Their preferred territories span altitudes of 2,500-4,000 metres across active volcanic ranges.

## Diet

These dragons are carnivorous and highly specialized, hunting thermal-loving lizards and heat-resistant arthropods found in volcanic regions. They occasionally venture to cooler elevations to hunt larger prey, displaying opportunistic feeding patterns during seasonal migrations.

## Behaviour

Emberwing Drakes are predominantly solitary, tolerating others only during breeding seasons. They utilize thermal updrafts for hunting, soaring for hours with minimal wing effort. Their vocalizations consist of low-frequency rumbles and occasional flame bursts used for territorial communication.

## Threat Level

**3 out of 5** — While capable of inflicting significant damage through aerial strikes and flame attacks, Emberwing Drakes show minimal aggression toward humanoids unless their nesting sites are directly threatened. Conservation concern remains moderate due to habitat loss from increased geothermal exploitation.
