## Summary

Create 3 breaking news articles for the Three-Body Survival Gazette. Each article is 80-150 words in inverted pyramid newspaper style with a headline.

## File to Create

`test-output/test-agentic-sdlc-three-body-gazette/breaking-news.md`

## Branch

`e01-s02/breaking-news` (from `epic/e01-news-weather`)

## Content Requirements

1. **Fleet departure ceremony** -- Report on the Trisolaran invasion fleet departure. Include: number of ships (~1,000), official quotes from the Fleet Commander, ceremony description (flags, speeches, synchronized dehydration salutes), estimated 450-stellar-cycle journey time. Tone: patriotic, proud, factual.
2. **Sophon deployment milestone** -- Science/tech report on the first successful 11-dimensional fold of a proton into a Sophon. Include: number of failed attempts, quote from lead Sophon Engineer, what the Sophon does (monitor Earth, disrupt particle accelerators), the unfolding process (2D plane visible from orbit). Tone: scientific achievement, national pride.
3. **Premature rehydration chaos** -- A Chaotic Era ended 3 minutes after the dehydration order. Mass confusion. Include: number of citizens affected, emergency services quotes, "fastest Chaotic Era in recorded history," rehydration injuries ("fiber tearing" during rapid rehydration).

Each article needs a newspaper-style headline.

## Format Contract

| Element | Exact Format | Example |
|---------|-------------|---------|
| Sun references | Always "Sun-1", "Sun-2", or "Sun-3" | `Sun-1 maintaining stable position` |
| Time units | "stellar time" for clock, "stellar cycles" for years | `450-stellar-cycle journey` |
| Headlines | Title Case, newspaper-style | `Fleet Departure Ceremony Marks Historic Day` |
| Item separator | `---` between articles | |

## Voice and Tone

Deadpan journalistic. Inverted pyramid structure (most important info first). Treat synchronized dehydration salutes and fiber tearing injuries with the same professional authority as any newspaper covers a national event or medical incident.

## Three-Body Universe Reference

- **Three suns:** Sun-1, Sun-2, Sun-3. Never "the Sun" singular.
- **Fleet:** ~1,000 ships, ~450 stellar cycle journey to Earth.
- **Sophons:** 11-dimensional proton fold (not 9, not 7 -- exactly 11). Unfolded into 2D plane. Deployed to Earth to disrupt particle accelerators and monitor humans.
- **Dehydration:** Trisolarans remove all water, collapse into flat husks. Rehydration is disorienting. Rapid rehydration causes "fiber tearing."
- **Time:** "stellar time" (hours), "stellar cycles" (years).
