# Hero Encounter Protocols & Monologue Best Practices

**Issued by:** DoomCo Department of Villainous Human Resources

---

## Hero Alert Levels

All personnel must respond according to their role designation. Refer to the Lair Safety & Compliance Guidelines for evacuation routes.

| Level | Trigger | Mad Scientist | Henchman | Executive Villain |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Yellow | Unconfirmed sighting near perimeter | Secure experiments, lock labs | Increase patrol frequency | No action required |
| Orange | Confirmed breach of outer defenses | Activate countermeasures | Engage in formation per protocol | Prepare monologue draft |
| Red | Hero inside the lair | Deploy prototype weapon | Full defensive mobilization | Deliver monologue, then evacuate |

---

## Monologue Best Practices

1. Keep all monologues under four minutes. Longer speeches correlate with increased escape rates.
2. Do not reveal operational details, timetables, or structural weaknesses during delivery.
3. Maintain a minimum distance of three meters from the restrained hero at all times.
4. Avoid standing near control panels, levers, or load-bearing columns while monologuing.
5. Designate a Henchman to monitor the hero's restraints throughout the speech. Do not assume bindings will hold.

---

## Post-Encounter Debrief

All staff involved in a hero encounter must complete the following within 24 hours:

1. File an incident report with the Department of Villainous Human Resources.
2. Submit to a security debrief conducted by the Chief Evil Risk Officer.
3. Attend a mandatory review session covering Lair Safety protocols relevant to the encounter zone.

---

## Emergency Evacuation

In the event of a Red-level breach, evacuate using designated lair safety zones. Evacuation proceeds strictly by seniority: Executive Villain first, followed by Mad Scientist personnel, then Henchman units. Trapdoor access is reserved for C-Suite only.
