## Summary

Create 3 classified ad sections for the Three-Body Survival Gazette: Real Estate, Employment, and Personals. Each section is 80-150 words in standard classified ad format.

## File to Create

`test-output/test-agentic-sdlc-three-body-gazette/classifieds.md`

## Branch

`e02-s01/classifieds` (from `epic/e02-community-lifestyle`)

## Content Requirements

1. **Real Estate** -- 3-4 property listings. Include: location, size, price in credits, key selling points. At least one listing mentions "survived X Chaotic Eras." One listing for a deep-crust shelter ("ideal for syzygy season"). Use Trisolaran real estate abbreviations: "3br/2ba, dehy-storage rm, reinf. foundation."
2. **Employment** -- 3-4 job postings. Include: Sophon operator (must tolerate 11-dimensional folding, competitive dehydration benefits), Fleet maintenance technician (must dehydrate for 450-stellar-cycle journey), Rehydration facility technician (strong stomach required). Standard job format with qualifications and benefits.
3. **Personals** -- 3-4 personal ads. Include: seeking partner for mutual rehydration (must own backup dehydration chamber), lost dehydrated family member (Warehouse 7, Section 3), seeking rehydration buddy. Ads are brutally honest (Trisolaran transparency -- they cannot lie).

## Format Contract

| Element | Exact Format | Example |
|---------|-------------|---------|
| Sun references | Always "Sun-1", "Sun-2", or "Sun-3" | `Sun-1 exposure: minimal` |
| Currency | "credits" (lowercase) | `Price: 45,000 credits` |
| Section headers | H2 with section name in caps | `## REAL ESTATE` |
| Item separator | `---` between sections | |

## Voice and Tone

Deadpan classified ad style. Abbreviated, efficient. The humor comes from applying standard classified ad format to Trisolaran content. Personals are completely transparent -- Trisolarans cannot lie.

## Three-Body Universe Reference

- **Three suns:** Sun-1, Sun-2, Sun-3. Never "the Sun" singular.
- **Dehydration:** Survival mechanism, stored in warehouses, government-ordered during Chaotic Eras.
- **Sophons:** 11-dimensional proton fold. Operators must tolerate higher-dimensional work.
- **Fleet:** ~450 stellar cycle journey. Long-haul positions require dehydration.
- **Culture:** Transparent communication. No lying, no euphemism. Direct and honest.
- **Currency:** "credits."
