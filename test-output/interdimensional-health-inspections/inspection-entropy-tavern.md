## Incident Summary

On 2026-03-12, a thermodynamic event caused temporal reversal affecting three tables. Patrons served the "Second Law Soufflé" within a reverse-entropy pocket un-ate their meals, which un-cooked themselves and reverted to raw ingredients that achieved locomotion and exited the kitchen.

Classified EMERGENCY pursuant to MFSA 300.1. Precedent: the Void Kitchen shutdown (MFSA-INS-2026-005), where three patrons ceased to exist during dining. Both cases involve involuntary patron displacement, though here temporal rather than existential.

## Root Cause Analysis

Management constructed a "Reverse-Entropy Alcove" exploiting localised entropy reversal as a novelty. Serving a soufflé -- dependent on entropy-driven reactions -- within this pocket triggered cascading temporal inversion. Staff lacked reverse-entropy training and held no entropy-variant certification.

## Regulatory Gap Analysis

1. **MFSA 88.4 (Ingredient Sourcing):** Does not address sentience acquired through temporal reversal. Evidence E-003 has no classification.
2. **MFSA 112.1 (Patron Continuity):** Requires patrons to "remain in existence" but does not specify temporal frame. The Void Kitchen precedent (MFSA-INS-2026-005) addressed existential displacement only.
3. **MFSA 300.1 (Temporal Displacement):** Lacks provisions for entropy-driven reversal specifically.
4. **No regulation** addresses food that un-cooks itself or achieves motility.

## Recommendations for Regulatory Amendment

1. **Proposed MFSA 312.1 (Reverse-Entropy Preparation):** No establishment shall serve food within entropy reversal zones without temporal shielding, Entropy-Variant Certification, and signed patron waivers.
2. **Proposed MFSA 312.2 (Ingredient Motility):** Any ingredient achieving locomotion or sentience during preparation shall be reclassified as a patron under MFSA 112.1.
3. **Proposed Amendment to MFSA 112.1:** Expand "remain in existence" to "remain in the current temporal frame and ontological state during dining, including dessert."

## Disposition

The Entropy Tavern is placed under provisional suspension pending temporal shielding installation and staff certification. The Reverse-Entropy Alcove is sealed by order of this Authority.
