# Formal Recommendation Re: Sol-3 (Earth) Disposition

**Memorandum #7741-CR** | Grand Researcher Vorthanix Qel, Director of Pre-Spacefaring Civilisation Affairs

---

## Executive Summary

Observation of *Homo sapiens* reveals a species of modest intelligence yet notable behavioural unpredictability (see Assessment #7741-SA). Field operatives documented ritualised phenomena defying standard predictive modelling (see Report #7741-C).

## Options Analysis

**(a) Initiate Contact.** Premature engagement risks observational contamination. Given the species' compulsion to document anomalous events[^1], covert operations would become untenable within hours.

**(b) Quarantine.** A full interdiction perimeter requires disproportionate fleet allocation for a species rated 4.2 on the sentience index. Expenditure cannot be justified at this threat level.

**(c) Nature Reserve.** The Fauna Preservation Act permits continued study while restricting unauthorised contact, consistent with existing infrastructure. Bounded observation permits assessment of developmental trajectory.

## Recommendation

This office recommends Option (c): nature reserve designation. The species presents negligible strategic threat yet offers considerable research value[^2]. Passive observation serves the Council's scientific mandate without resource burden.
