# Episode: The Open-Plan Migration

As spring descends upon the corporate landscape, the herd instinctively senses the approaching reorganisation. Management's decree reverberates through the cubicles: *restructuring*. The worker ants must migrate. Here, clutching monitor arms and personal mugs with practiced devotion, they traverse the fluorescent plains toward their new territorial stations. The seasonal desk-moving behaviour of *Officius reorganisatus* is a marvel of social choreography — families of colleagues shuffle in synchronised clusters, their prized possessions — novelty mugs, framed photographs, desk plants — held aloft like treasures being evacuated from a sinking ship.

[*Camera pans across the warren of empty desk cavities.*]

The herd moves with hushed resignation, each member marking their new territory with the ceremonial placement of family photos and motivational quote magnets. This annual migration, triggered by management reshuffling, reveals the unspoken pecking order. The corner desk seekers assert dominance. The quiet ones claim the window niches. No words are exchanged — only the soft percussion of tape dispensers and cable ties. Within hours, the open plan has been reborn, identical yet transformed, a landscape forever altered by the rhythms of corporate seasonal change.
