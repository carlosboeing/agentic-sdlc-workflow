# The Great Fire of London — London, England

**Rating:** ★☆☆☆☆
**Reviewer:** ChronoNomad_42
**Date Visited:** 2–5 September 1666
**Date Reviewed:** 15 March 2026
**Trip Type:** Solo

---

## Review

I booked the "Historic London Weekend Getaway" for what the brochure called a "vibrant, bustling city experience." What I got was approximately 80% of the city reduced to ash and a personal inventory of singed eyebrows.

The fire safety infrastructure here is essentially nonexistent. No sprinklers, no evacuation routes, no emergency signage. When I asked a city official about the evacuation plan, he handed me a bucket and pointed toward the Thames.

The smell was indescribable — burning timber, melting tallow, and every bakery in the city combusting at once. Speaking of bakeries: I had read the reviews on the Pudding Lane establishment before my trip and they were *already* terrible. I should have taken that as an omen. The fire started there on 2 September and did not stop until it consumed Cheapside, Guildhall, and most of the Cathedral.

The crowds fleeing with furniture and livestock made navigation impossible. Zero stars for foot traffic management.

I will concede one point, begrudgingly: the view of the flames reflected across the Thames at night was admittedly spectacular.

Would not recommend. Will not return.
