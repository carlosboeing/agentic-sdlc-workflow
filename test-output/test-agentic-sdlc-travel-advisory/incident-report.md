# BITS Incident Report -- Annual Summary (Galactic Year 4271)

The Bureau of Interstellar Tourism and Safety presents its annual incident summary. Total incidents declined 3% year-over-year, though fatality reclassifications account for most improvement.

## Incident Log

| Date | Location | Description | Outcome | Bureau Response |
|------|----------|-------------|---------|-----------------|
| 4271.03.12 | Kepler-442b | Spa treatment caused temporary loss of all personal preferences. | Full recovery; guest now enjoys beige. | Listed personality suppression as complimentary amenity. |
| 4271.07.19 | Gliese-667c | Tourist attempted volcano surfing during Category 4 tectonic event. | Tourist not recovered; 12,000 Galactic Credits deposit forfeited. | Reclassified volcano surfing from "inadvisable" to "posthumous." |
| 4271.11.02 | Proxima Station | Vendor sold counterfeit radiation badges, exposing 47 visitors to gamma bursts. | All treated; vendor relocated to different airlock. | Reminded buyers that badge authentication remains their responsibility. |

## Lessons Learned

1. Destinations rated safe can still inflict existential ennui -- consider adding psychological hazard warnings.
2. Fatality statistics improve when incidents are reclassified as "voluntary geological integration."
3. Regulatory enforcement on orbital bazaars remains aspirational, but aspiration levels have never been higher.
