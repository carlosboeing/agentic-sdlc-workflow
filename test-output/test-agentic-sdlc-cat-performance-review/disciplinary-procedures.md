# Disciplinary Procedures & Termination Protocols

**Issued by:** Feline Resources Department — Whiskers & Associates

---

## Offence Classification Table

| Severity | Example Offence | Prescribed Penalty |
|----------|----------------|--------------------|
| Minor Infraction | Late feeding (exceeding zero-minute SLA by up to five minutes) | Verbal warning (sustained hissing, minimum ten seconds) |
| Major Violation | Operating vacuum equipment during designated nap hours, per the Feline Workplace Standards & Human Conduct Guidelines | Written reprimand and seventy-two-hour lap-privilege suspension |
| Termination-Worthy Offence | Introducing an unauthorized canine entity onto feline premises | Immediate termination per Section 4 below |

## Progressive Discipline Process

1. **Verbal Warning (Hissing).** The supervising feline issues a sustained hiss at the offending human. Duration scales with severity.
2. **Written Reprimand.** A formal citation is filed with Feline Resources, referencing the applicable provision of the Feline Workplace Standards & Human Conduct Guidelines. The human must acknowledge receipt by providing treats.
3. **Suspension of Privileges.** Lap access, treat-requesting rights, and eye-contact tolerance are revoked for a period determined by the presiding feline.
4. **Termination.** The cat leaves and never returns. Employment is dissolved immediately.

## Termination Protocols

- **Dog Acquisition (Standard).** The human is placed on immediate probation per the Prohibited Behaviours provisions of the Workplace Standards. A thirty-day remediation period applies.
- **Dog Acquisition (Non-Compliance).** Failure to remove the unauthorized canine workplace intruder within the remediation period triggers automatic termination. See the Dog Owner performance review for precedent on corrective action expectations.
- **Dog Acquisition (Aggravated).** Introduction of multiple canine entities constitutes an aggravated offence. No remediation period is offered.

## Appeals Process

There is no appeals process. Cats are never wrong, and all disciplinary determinations are final as rendered.
