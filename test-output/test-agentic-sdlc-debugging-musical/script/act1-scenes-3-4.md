# Act I, Scene 3: The Merge Conflict

*The CtrlZ office, same morning. Main branch still broken from the standup. MARCUS and ZOE both push to `auth-service.js` at once.*

**Characters present:** Marcus Chen, Zoe Kim, Priya Mehta, Aisha Okafor, Riley Park

---

**MARCUS:** Quick refactor. Done.

**ZOE:** Quick fix. Done.

*Both get the same error. Tango lighting floods the stage.*

---

### Song: "The Merge Conflict"

**Performed by:** Marcus Chen and Zoe Kim (duet)
**Style:** Tango, dramatic tension
**Key:** D minor
**Tempo:** 112 BPM (Allegretto)

**[VERSE 1]**
MARCUS: You rewrote my carefully crafted lines —
ZOE: You left your footprints running all through mine —
BOTH: The diff won't close, the arrows won't align,
Jordan pitched this code this morning and it's looking less than fine.

**[CHORUS]**
BOTH: Conflict in the codebase, conflict in our hearts,
Git says we've diverged right from the start —
Accept current? Accept incoming? No winning here —
Every line belongs to both of us, and neither, I fear.

**[BRIDGE]**
*They orbit each other with laptops raised.*
BOTH: Main was already broken at the standup — now it's worse.

**[FINAL CHORUS / TAG]**
BOTH: Conflict! No clean resolution —
Push to production, pray for absolution!

*Half the team behind Marcus, half behind Zoe. AISHA opens her "told you so" spreadsheet.*

---

### Song: "Rebase or Rewrite?"

**Performed by:** Full cast (ensemble)
**Style:** Rock anthem, call-and-response
**Key:** E major
**Tempo:** 140 BPM (Allegro)

**[CHORUS — call-and-response]**
MARCUS SIDE: REBASE!
ZOE SIDE: REWRITE!
BOTH: Someone has to choose — and either way we lose!

**[VERSE 1]**
MARCUS SIDE: Keep the history — every commit matters!
ZOE SIDE: The log's all "wip" and "oops" — burn it till it scatters!

**[BRIDGE — Aisha, deadpan]**
I filed a ticket. Q3. Priority: Low.

**[FINAL CHORUS / TAG]**
ALL: REBASE! REWRITE! — *[music cuts]*

*Silence. The conflict is "resolved." RILEY has not moved.*

# Act I, Scene 4: The Intern

*RILEY stares at a sticky note on the monitor: "Day 1 task: just quickly fix the CI pipeline." A guitar begins.*

**Characters present:** Riley Park

---

### Song: "The Intern's Lament"

**Performed by:** Riley Park (solo)
**Style:** Slow ballad, acoustic guitar
**Key:** G major
**Tempo:** 58 BPM (Larghetto)

**[VERSE 1]**
They said "just quickly fix it" on the morning I arrived —
Six months of flaky tests and missing ENV_KEYs survived.
Docker builds that work locally but die in CI —
I'm still the intern, and the pipeline still asks why.

**[CHORUS]**
The tests are flaky, the environment's a lie,
The secret variables are secrets from the CI.
Jordan pitched this code this morning — almost made it fly —
Still the intern. Still fixing the CI.

**[BRIDGE]**
The merge conflict, the rebase war — none of it was mine —
But forty-seven seconds I watched the green light shine —
Failed at three a.m. I rebuilt the line.

**[FINAL CHORUS / TAG]**
Still the intern.
Still fixing it.
Still.

*The guitar fades. RILEY opens another Stack Overflow tab.*
